**Episodes:** EP001–EP006  
**Audience:** 2–4 years  
**Language:** Indonesian  
**Status:** SCRIPT MASTER V01  
**Date:** 2026-09-12

## GLOBAL SCRIPT LOCK

Canonical opening, verbatim:

> “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

Canonical closing, verbatim:

> “Hebat sekali hari ini!”
>
> “Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”
>
> “Belajar... Bermain... Berbagi... Bersama Rere!”
>
> “Sampai jumpa, teman-teman! Dadah!”

Interaction loop:

**Rere → Question → PAUSE → Child response → Rere confirmation → Repeat**

CTA is optional, brief, and never allowed to interrupt a learning interaction.

# EP001 — RERE DAN PETUALANGAN WARNA

**Promise:** mengenali merah, kuning, biru, hijau, pink.  
**Pillar:** DISCOVER  
**Story:** Rere ingin menggambar dan mencari warna.

### S01 Opening
RERE: “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

[PAUSE 3–5 sec]

### S04 Yellow

### S05 Blue

[PAUSE 3–5 sec]

### S06 Green
RERE: “Rere melihat tanaman! Daunnya warna apa?”
[PAUSE 3–5 sec]
RERE: “HIJAU! Ayo bilang... hijau.”

### S07 Pink
RERE: “Waaaah! Rere menemukan warna kesukaan Rere! Teman-teman tahu warna apa?”
[PAUSE 3–5 sec]
RERE: “PINK! Rere suka pink!”

### S08 Quiz
RERE: “Mana yang warna MERAH?” [PAUSE]
RERE: “Betul!”
RERE: “Mana yang warna KUNING?” [PAUSE]
RERE: “Betul!”
RERE: “Yang mana warna PINK?” [PAUSE]

### S10 Review
