# EP004 — RERE BERMAIN COCOKKAN

**Storyboard V01 — Production Lock Draft**  
**Target:** ages 2–4, 3–4 minutes  
**Pillar:** DISCOVER  
**Learning promise:** menemukan pasangan identik dengan satu ciri yang jelas.

## Canonical Sources
Canonical Rere character/face/outfit; `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`; canonical matching learning sheet; three stable object pairs.

## Shot Plan

### S01 — Opening | 0:00–0:12
Centered Rere, canonical greeting. FS, child-height.

### S02 — Lost Partners | 0:12–0:28
Three objects visibly separated from their identical partners. Rere: “Hmm... ada benda yang kehilangan pasangannya. Yuk kita cari!”

### S03 — Pair 1 | 0:28–0:50
One ball and two candidate areas, only one identical counterpart. Rere: “Ini satu bola. Mana pasangannya?” PAUSE 4 sec. Then “Ketemu! Cocok!”

### S04 — Pair 2 | 0:50–1:12
One block with one visually obvious identical partner. “Ini satu balok. Mana yang sama?” PAUSE. “Betul! Mereka cocok.”

### S05 — Pair 3 | 1:12–1:34
One shape card and its identical partner. “Mana pasangannya?” PAUSE. “Yeay! Ketemu!”

### S06 — Matching Rule | 1:34–2:00
All three completed pairs. Rere demonstrates: “Kalau sama, kita pasangkan.” Slow physical matching motion.

### S07 — Child Participation | 2:00–2:42
Rere asks child to find one matching pair nearby. PAUSE 4–5 sec. Encourage without assuming what object child chose.

### S08 — Review | 2:42–3:10
Completed pairs remain visible. “Sama... cocok... pasangan.”

### S09 — Closing | 3:10–3:45
Canonical closing/end card.

## Gemini Constraints
One matching criterion only: identity/sameness. Ensure exactly one plausible answer per question. Reject ambiguous pairs, accidental duplicates, object redesign, character/outfit drift, clutter, text artifacts and overstimulation.
