# RERE BATCH 001 — LEARNING REVIEW V01

**Status:** Production Standard  
**Episodes:** EP001–EP006  
**Date:** 2026-09-12

## 1. Purpose

Turn Batch 001 results into deliberate improvements for Batch 002 without destabilizing the Rere identity system.

## 2. Review Layers

### Layer A — Production

- render defects
- continuity defects
- voice/audio defects
- editing defects
- packaging defects

### Layer B — Learning

- objective clarity
- child participation
- vocabulary repetition
- task difficulty
- PAUSE effectiveness
- progression suitability

### Layer C — Audience

- topic interest
- retention
- rewatch behavior
- parent feedback
- comments/questions
- traffic source patterns

## 3. Hypothesis Log

Every experiment should state:

- hypothesis
- variable changed
- episodes affected
- expected result
- observed result
- decision

Example:

> Hypothesis: a faster visual hook increases early retention without reducing comprehension.

Do not change several variables and then claim one caused the result.

## 4. Metric Interpretation

Metrics are signals, not automatic diagnoses.

Low retention may result from:

- weak hook
- confusing scene
- long explanation
- insufficient visual clarity
- inappropriate pacing
- topic mismatch

Low click-through may result from:

- weak packaging
- unclear promise
- poor topic-market fit

Always inspect the actual episode before changing the system.

## 5. Child-Centered Review

Where possible, conduct structured observation with appropriate parental consent and safeguards.

Observe:

- what children look at first
- whether they answer during PAUSE
- whether they imitate vocabulary
- whether they stay engaged
- whether they request repetition
- where confusion appears

Do not treat one child's behavior as universal evidence.

## 6. Parent Feedback

Classify feedback into:

- learning value
- age suitability
- character appeal
- clarity
- audio
- pacing
- safety/trust
- technical quality

Look for patterns rather than isolated comments.

## 7. Batch Decision Rules

### KEEP
Works and should remain standard.

### IMPROVE
Works but has identifiable friction.

### TEST
Promising but requires controlled experiment.

### RETIRE
Repeatedly underperforms or creates confusion.

### PROTECT
Canonical elements that must not be altered for performance reasons.

## 8. What We Protect

Always protect:

- Rere identity
- canonical voice identity
- canonical recurring props
- canonical world identity
- child-safe tone
- learning-first philosophy

## 9. Batch 002 Inputs

After review, create:

- story-format recommendations
- pacing recommendations
- packaging experiments
- asset-library additions
- production efficiency improvements
