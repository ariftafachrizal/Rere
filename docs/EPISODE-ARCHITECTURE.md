# Rere — Episode Architecture

**Status:** Working Standard — 2026-09-11

## Standard Episode Structure

Rere episodes use a five-part macro structure.

### Scene 1 — Signature Intro

Target duration: 8–10 seconds.

Locked opening:

> **"Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!"**

The opening should remain visually and verbally consistent across episodes.

### Scene 2 — Theme / Story Hook

Target duration: 5–10 seconds.

Do not use a long static title card as the primary device. Introduce the theme through a small story situation, discovery, question, or object that naturally leads into the episode topic.

Example:

> Rere finds a box of crayons.
> 
> "Wah... ada banyak warna!"
> 
> "Hari ini kita cari tahu warna, yuk!"

The episode title may appear as an overlay while the story hook continues.

### Scene 3 — Short CTA

Target duration: approximately 5–7 seconds maximum.

Because the primary audience is children, CTA must not feel like an adult marketing interruption.

Preferred approach:

- very short
- child-safe
- no long list of actions
- do not say “like, comment, subscribe, activate bell” all at once
- preserve learning momentum
- visual CTA may be enough

Working recommendation:

> "Kalau suka main dan belajar bersama Rere, jangan lupa subscribe ya!"
>
> "Sekarang... yuk kita mulai!"

Alternative preferred child-first treatment: keep spoken CTA to a minimum and show a subtle subscribe overlay while Rere transitions into the activity.

### Scene 4 — Learning Story

This is the main part of the episode.

Use:

**STORY → DISCOVERY → QUESTION → PAUSE → ANSWER/ACTION → POSITIVE REINFORCEMENT → REPETITION WITH VARIATION**

The child should receive opportunities to:

- observe
- predict
- answer
- point
- imitate
- move
- repeat
- recall

Avoid turning the scene into a lecture.

### Scene 5 — Signature Closing

Target duration: 10–15 seconds.

Use the approved closing asset and dialogue consistently.

Working closing dialogue:

> “Hebat sekali hari ini!”
>
> “Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”
>
> “Belajar...”
> 
> “Bermain...”
>
> “Berbagi...”
>
> “Bersama Rere!”
>
> “Sampai jumpa, teman-teman!”
>
> “Dadah!”

End card:

**BELAJAR • BERMAIN • BERBAGI**  
**BERSAMA RERE!**

## Learning Design Rules

One episode should have **one clear primary learning objective** even when supporting outcomes are included.

For young children, prefer fewer concepts with:

- more contextual examples
- repetition with variation
- interaction
- pause/wait time
- emotional reward

## Age Adaptation

### 1–2 years

- very short phrases
- strong repetition
- fewer concepts
- more gesture/action
- simple object recognition
- minimal verbal explanation

### 2–4 years

Default Rere production target.

- simple story
- questions and pauses
- basic classification/counting/language
- short mini-games
- concrete examples

### 4–5 years

- slightly richer narrative
- simple reasoning
- sequencing
- prediction
- more functional phrases
- slightly longer interactive challenges

## Recommended Duration

The exact duration can vary by topic and platform, but the production target should favor **high signal over unnecessary runtime**.

Do not add filler just to reach a duration target.

## Content Priority

A useful hierarchy is:

**Learning objective → Story → Interaction → Character consistency → Visual entertainment → CTA optimization**

The CTA must never displace the core learning experience.
