### Safety/QA
Letters must be visually legible and not depend on tiny text. EP085–086 teach listening rather than formal phonics mastery. EP090 mappings must be unambiguous. Drawing activities use large child-safe crayons and paper; no sharp tools. Problem-solving episodes provide two safe practical choices and never punish the wrong choice. Story sequencing uses exactly three distinct visual events. Avoid pressure to read or perform perfectly.

### Gemini contract
Use canonical Rere → environment → relevant art/learning props → objective → action → camera → lighting → motion → continuity → negative → QA. Spoken dialogue remains Indonesian and short. Text shown on props must be generated only when necessary; learning must remain understandable without text.
