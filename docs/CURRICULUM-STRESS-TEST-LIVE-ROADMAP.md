## Key Findings

1. **No structural gap:** Values, life skills, safety, responsibility, and healthy habits fit without creating additional pillars.
2. **Safety remains distinct:** Safety can require firm adult boundaries while still avoiding fear-based storytelling.
3. **Independence is gradual:** The roadmap supports scaffolding instead of unrealistic self-sufficiency.
4. **Agency is preserved:** Children can choose within safe boundaries and may say no to non-safety physical contact.
5. **Cross-pillar boundaries remain clear:** language → SPEAK; emotion → FEEL; understanding cause → DISCOVER; creative expression → CREATE.
6. **Real-life relevance is strong:** The roadmap works particularly well for recurring daily experiences.

## Verdict

**PASS — no structural change required.**

LIVE is broad enough for ages 2–4 and scalable toward age 5 through increasing routine complexity, context variation, independence, reasoning, and social contribution.

## Guardrail

Redesign any episode that relies primarily on fear, shame, threats, obedience, forced affection, perfectionism, or unsafe independence expectations.
