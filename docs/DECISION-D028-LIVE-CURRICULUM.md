# D-028 — LIVE Curriculum

**Status:** Approved — 2026-09-12

## Decision

LIVE is formally established as the fourth Core Pillar of Rere's five-pillar learning architecture:

> **LIVE — Care for Myself, Others & My World**

## Developmental Spine

> **NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY**

## Core Areas

1. Values & Respect
2. Daily Life Skills & Independence
3. Safety

Responsibility & contribution, healthy habits & well-being, agency, persistence, flexibility, and cooperation remain supporting capabilities rather than additional pillars.

## Three-Year Arc

- **Year 1 — Participate in Everyday Life:** NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE.
- **Year 2 — Contribute with Purpose:** PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE.
- **Year 3 — Apply & Take Increasing Responsibility:** PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY.

## Key Principles

- Meaning before obedience.
- Model before demand.
- Safety is firm; other values can allow choice.
- Independence is scaffolded.
- Practice in real contexts.
- Respect body and boundaries.
- Repetition with variation.

## Stress-Test Result

A 40-case stress test covering hygiene, independence, cleanup, sharing/boundaries, helping, road/home/public safety, emergency situations, refusal, mistakes, multilingual/nonverbal participation, pretend play, family variation, and anti-patterns produced **PASS** with no structural changes required.

## Consequence

LIVE is ready as a stable curriculum pillar. Future episodes must use the Primary Learning Intention rule and must not turn LIVE into obedience training, fear-based safety education, or unrealistic independence training.
