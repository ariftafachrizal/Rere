# Rere — SPEAK Three-Year Roadmap Stress Test

**Status:** PASS — 2026-09-12

## Purpose

Stress-test the three-year SPEAK curriculum roadmap against realistic edge cases before treating it as an approved working roadmap.

## Test Criteria

A case passes when the roadmap can accommodate it without:
- changing the six-stage SPEAK progression;
- creating a new learning strand unnecessarily;
- turning English into the sole definition of communication;
- forcing verbal production;
- confusing communication with another pillar's primary intention;
- requiring a rigid age gate;
- relying on vocabulary quantity as the main progression measure.

## Stress-Test Findings

### 1. No structural gap

All 40 cases fit the roadmap without adding a new stage or strand.

### 2. Multilingual design remains coherent

The roadmap does not require English-only participation. This is essential because communication development and English development are related but not identical.

### 3. Nonverbal participation is protected

Pointing, gesture, action, selection, facial expression, and other meaningful responses remain valid, especially in earlier developmental stages.

### 4. Progression is multidimensional

The strongest progression signal is not vocabulary count. Context complexity, response complexity, interaction complexity, and language flexibility can all increase independently.

### 5. Pillar boundaries remain clear

The same topic can belong to different pillars. The deciding factor remains the primary learning intention.

### 6. Story and music boundaries remain usable

Storytelling belongs to SPEAK when communication/comprehension is primary and to CREATE when invention/creative expression is primary. Music/rhythm can similarly be SPEAK or CREATE depending on intention.

### 7. Roadmap remains production-friendly

The roadmap is detailed enough to guide Episode Briefs but does not require a fixed three-year episode list. This preserves the agreed workflow: roadmap → journey/chapter → episode experience.

## Verdict

**PASS — no structural change required.**

The SPEAK Three-Year Curriculum Roadmap is approved as a working curriculum map, subject to future evidence, production learning, child-development observations, safety review, and audience learning.

## Final Rule

> **Do not measure SPEAK progress by how much language a child can repeat. Measure whether the child increasingly understands and uses communication to participate, express meaning, interact, and accomplish things with others.**
