# Rere — Lighting & Color Grading Bible

**Status:** Production Standard — v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Batch:** 04 — Lighting & Color Grading  
**Scope:** image generation, animation, video compositing, scene continuity, thumbnails, title cards, and post-production.

---

## 1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mengunci bahasa cahaya, exposure, contrast, saturation, color temperature, skin rendering, dan color grading di universe Rere.

Tujuannya bukan membuat semua scene memiliki pencahayaan yang sama. Tujuannya adalah memastikan **mood boleh berubah tanpa membuat dunia Rere terasa berubah identitas**.

> **Lighting changes the mood. Color grading preserves the world.**

---

## 2. Relationship to Other Canonical Systems

Production hierarchy:

**Identity → Face → Anatomy → Outfit → Gesture/Expression → World → Props → Camera → Lighting → Scene Details**

Lighting dan grading tidak boleh mengubah:

- warna kulit Rere secara ekstrem
- warna mata
- warna rambut
- warna canonical outfit
- warna identity props
- bentuk/reading objek
- visual DNA world

Jika lighting membuat sebuah canonical asset sulit dikenali, lighting harus dikoreksi.

---

## 3. Rere Lighting DNA

Default visual feel:

- warm
- soft
- diffused
- bright but gentle
- clean
- cheerful
- dimensional
- low visual aggression
- child-safe
- polished 3D CGI

Cahaya harus terasa seperti **dunia nyata yang dibuat lebih lembut dan ramah untuk anak**, bukan studio iklan yang terlalu glossy atau film yang terlalu dramatic.

---

## 4. Master Lighting Rule

> **Soft key + gentle fill + controlled rim + readable background.**

Default setup:

1. **Key light** — sumber cahaya utama, soft.
2. **Fill light** — menjaga wajah dan objek tidak kehilangan detail.
3. **Rim/separation light** — optional, sangat halus.
4. **Ambient/world light** — menjaga environment tetap readable.

Tidak diperlukan lighting setup kompleks bila tidak membantu cerita.

---

## 5. Exposure Standard

Target:

- wajah selalu readable
- highlight tidak clipping secara berlebihan
- shadow tidak crushed
- white/cream tidak berubah menjadi abu-abu kotor
- pastel tetap memiliki separation
- pink tetap identifiable

Hindari:

- blown-out face
- pure white highlight pada sebagian besar frame
- crushed black shadows
- underexposed preschool scenes
- extreme HDR look
- artificial plastic shine yang berlebihan

Priority:

**Readability > Mood > Realism > Stylization Detail**

---

## 6. Contrast Standard

Default contrast: **low to medium**.

Gunakan contrast lebih rendah untuk:

- toddler learning
- emotional safety
- bedtime
- quiet storytelling

Contrast dapat sedikit dinaikkan untuk:

- outdoor play
- discovery
- adventure
- imagination climax

Tetap hindari:

- horror-like contrast
- noir shadows
- extreme cinematic blacks
- aggressive chiaroscuro

---

## 7. Saturation Standard

Rere menggunakan warna yang ceria tetapi terkontrol.

Default:

**Pastel → Medium Saturation → Clean Separation**

Jangan membuat seluruh frame neon.

### Pink Rule

Pink adalah signature color, bukan global tint.

Pink harus:

- mudah dikenali pada Rere
- konsisten antar-shot
- tetap terlihat natural di berbagai lighting

Pink tidak boleh:

- berubah menjadi merah karena warm light
- berubah menjadi ungu karena cool light
- menjadi neon tanpa narrative reason
- mendominasi seluruh environment

---

## 8. Skin Tone Protection

Skin tone Rere harus tetap:

- warm natural
- healthy
- soft
- consistent

Color grading tidak boleh membuat kulit:

- terlalu orange
- terlalu pink/magenta
- terlalu pale/white
- grey/desaturated
- artificial red

Jika warm lighting diperlukan, gunakan environment warmth lebih dahulu daripada menggeser skin tone secara ekstrem.

---

## 9. Canonical Color Relationships

Core identity colors:

- Rere pink — signature accent
- warm cream/white — neutral foundation
- light wood — natural grounding
- pastel green — nature/life
- pastel blue — sky/water
- pastel yellow — energy/learning
- soft lavender — imagination/calm accent

Rule:

> **Pink identifies Rere; supporting colors identify the place and lesson.**

## 10. Time-of-Day Presets

### 10.1 Morning — `LIGHT-MORNING-01`

Feel:

- fresh
- optimistic
- soft golden daylight
- clean highlights
- gentle shadows

Use for:

- opening
- morning routine
- discovery
- first learning activity

### 10.2 Daytime — `LIGHT-DAY-01`

Default production lighting.

Feel:

- bright
- neutral-warm
- highly readable
- soft shadow

Use for most educational scenes.

### 10.3 Afternoon — `LIGHT-AFTERNOON-01`

Feel:

- slightly warmer
- relaxed
- playful
- still bright

Use for outdoor play, garden, community, transition.

### 10.4 Sunset — `LIGHT-SUNSET-01`

Feel:

- warm
- gentle
- emotional
- reflective

Use selectively for story transitions and emotional moments.

### 10.5 Night — `LIGHT-NIGHT-01`

Feel:

- cozy
- calm
- readable
- warm practical lights
- cool ambient window tones when appropriate

Use for bedtime and imagination scenes.

Never make preschool night scenes frightening.

---

## 11. Location Lighting Presets

### Playroom

Soft window daylight + gentle ambient fill.

### Bedroom

Soft warm daylight for morning; warm lamp/practical light for bedtime.

### Kitchen

Clean bright daylight, neutral-warm surfaces, readable food colors.

### Garden

Natural daylight, soft directional sun, green foliage readable without harsh highlights.

### Classroom

Bright diffused daylight, low shadow contrast, clear learning surfaces.

### Outdoor Play

Bright daylight, open-sky feel, controlled highlights.

### Community

Natural daylight, safe visibility, balanced street/building colors.

### Market

Warm daylight with colorful product readability; avoid oversaturation.

### Nature

Natural atmospheric light, water and foliage separation, soft depth.

### Imagination

Can use magical color and glow, but preserve Rere's face, skin, outfit, and recognizable props.

---

## 12. Imagination Lighting Rules

Imagination is the exception with the widest lighting range.

Allowed:

- soft magical glow
- stars
- moonlight
- colored ambient light
- dreamy bloom
- gentle volumetric light

Still prohibited:

- horror lighting
- aggressive neon wash
- face-obscuring colored light
- extreme bloom that removes details
- identity-changing color treatment

---

## 13. Shadow Language

Shadows should communicate depth, not danger.

Preferred:

- soft-edged shadows
- contact shadows
- gentle grounding
- subtle ambient occlusion

Avoid:

- hard black facial shadows
- ominous cast shadows
- giant threatening silhouettes
- visually confusing overlapping shadows

---

## 14. Highlights & Specular

3D materials should have controlled highlights.

Preferred:

- soft plastic highlight
- gentle fabric sheen
- natural wood response
- clean paper response

Avoid:

- mirror-like toys
- wet-looking skin
- excessive glossy surfaces
- blown-out pink objects

---

## 15. Depth & Atmosphere

Background may be slightly softer than Rere.

Use:

- subtle depth-of-field
- atmospheric perspective for wide nature scenes
- restrained background haze

Do not blur the learning object when it is the lesson target.

Learning priority:

**Rere + learning object > supporting prop > background.**

---

## 16. Learning Object Color Protection

When a lesson depends on color, the target object receives priority.

Example: if teaching **merah**, the red object must remain clearly red under the selected lighting.

Never let:

- warm grading turn yellow into orange
- cool grading turn blue into purple
- magenta lighting make pink indistinguishable from red

When necessary, simplify background colors around the learning object.

---

## 17. Emotional Lighting Modes

### Cheerful

Bright, warm, soft, open.

### Curious

Clean directional light with gentle focus separation.

### Calm

Lower contrast, soft warm ambience.

### Proud / Achievement

Bright key, subtle uplifting rim, clean background.

### Empathy / Sad-but-Safe

Softer contrast and reduced saturation; never gloomy or frightening.

### Excited

Bright, slightly higher energy, controlled saturation.

### Imaginative

Magical accent lighting while maintaining identity protection.

---

## 18. Camera + Lighting Interaction

Lighting must support the camera preset.

### Close-up

Protect facial detail and eye catchlights.

### Medium

Balance Rere and learning object.

### Full Body

Maintain floor/contact shadow and readable clothing colors.

### Wide

Prioritize environment readability without making Rere disappear.

### PAUSE / Question Shot

Keep face and target object especially readable; avoid distracting moving light or strong color effects.

---

## 19. Animation Lighting Continuity

Between adjacent shots, do not randomly change:

- sun direction
- color temperature
- exposure
- shadow direction
- background brightness
- Rere's skin tone
- canonical prop color

Small changes are acceptable when camera position physically implies them.

Large changes require a story/time/location transition.

---

## 20. Color Grading Pipeline

Recommended conceptual order:

1. Exposure normalization
2. White balance
3. Skin tone protection
4. Character color protection
5. Learning object protection
6. Environment balancing
7. Contrast shaping
8. Saturation control
9. Subtle mood treatment
10. Final consistency check

Do not apply a strong global LUT before identity protection.

---

## 21. Grade Presets

### `GRADE-RERE-BASE-01`

Default Rere universe grade:

- warm-neutral
- soft contrast
- clean whites
- pastel separation
- natural skin
- controlled pink

### `GRADE-RERE-LEARNING-01`

Maximum readability for educational concepts.

### `GRADE-RERE-COZY-01`

Warm, calm, lower contrast.

### `GRADE-RERE-OUTDOOR-01`

Fresh daylight, natural greens/blues, controlled highlights.

### `GRADE-RERE-IMAGINATION-01`

Dreamy color accents and soft glow, with identity protection.

---

## 22. White Balance Rules

Default indoor:

**neutral-warm**

Default outdoor:

**natural daylight with slight warmth**

Night:

**warm practical + restrained cool ambient**

Avoid strong green/magenta casts.

---

## 23. Rere Eye Catchlight

Rere's eyes are an important emotional readability feature.

Maintain:

- visible catchlight when appropriate
- natural eye depth
- brown eye identity
- no distracting multi-colored reflections

Catchlight can reflect environment but should not obscure the eye color.

---

## 24. Bloom & Glow

Bloom is optional and restrained.

Good uses:

- sunlight through window
- magical stars
- sunset
- small practical lamps

Bad uses:

- entire frame glowing
- face losing detail
- learning object losing edge definition
- excessive dreamy haze in normal learning scenes

---

## 25. Regular & Muslimah Compatibility

Lighting and grading must not differentiate the two presentation modes into separate visual universes.

Both should share:

- same skin rendering
- same pink identity
- same environment palette
- same softness
- same contrast philosophy

Hijab material and modest clothing must remain readable without becoming visually heavier than the rest of the design.

---

## 26. Thumbnail & Title Card Lighting

Thumbnail:

- brighter than normal scene when necessary for mobile readability
- face clear
- eyes readable
- key object clearly separated
- background simplified

Title card:

- clean
- bright
- high readability
- limited visual clutter

Do not over-grade thumbnails until the character colors become inaccurate.

## 28. Negative Prompt — Lighting & Grade Drift

```text
Do not use horror lighting.
Do not use extreme cinematic contrast.
Do not crush shadows.
Do not blow out faces or pastel surfaces.
Do not apply heavy neon color washes.
Do not turn Rere's skin orange, magenta, grey, or unnaturally pale.
Do not change brown eyes into another color.
Do not make pink neon unless explicitly required.
Do not tint the entire world pink.
Do not oversaturate the environment.
Do not let lighting obscure the learning object.
Do not randomly change white balance between adjacent shots.
Do not randomly reverse shadow direction.
Do not use excessive bloom or lens glow.
Do not create frightening silhouettes.
Do not let grading redefine canonical character or prop colors.
```

## 30. QA Checklist

### Character

- [ ] Skin tone natural and consistent.
- [ ] Brown eyes remain brown.
- [ ] Hair color remains canonical.
- [ ] Pink identity remains recognizable.
- [ ] Face remains readable.

### Props

- [ ] Canonical prop colors remain correct.
- [ ] Material remains readable.
- [ ] Highlights do not distort silhouette.

### Learning

- [ ] Target object is readable.
- [ ] Color lesson remains accurate.
- [ ] Text/educational graphics remain legible.

### Continuity

- [ ] Exposure consistent with adjacent shot.
- [ ] White balance consistent.
- [ ] Shadow direction consistent.
- [ ] Light intensity consistent.
- [ ] Time-of-day makes sense.

### World

- [ ] Supporting palette fits canonical world.
- [ ] Pink is accent, not global tint.
- [ ] Environment remains cheerful and child-safe.

### Final

- [ ] No accidental cinematic/horror mood.
- [ ] No overexposure.
- [ ] No crushed shadows.
- [ ] No excessive saturation.
- [ ] Rere remains the visual anchor.

---

## 31. Production Golden Rule

> **Change the light to change the feeling, not the identity.**

Rere may experience morning, afternoon, sunset, bedtime, rain, or magical imagination. The lighting can evolve with the story, but the viewer should always feel that they are looking at the **same Rere universe**.
