# Rere — Voice Bible

**Status:** Production Standard — v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Batch:** 05 — Voice Identity  
**Scope:** dialogue, narration, AI voice generation, voice direction, dubbing, editing, multilingual adaptation, and recurring audio identity.

---

## 1. Purpose

Voice Bible mengunci identitas suara Rere agar karakter terdengar sebagai **Rere yang sama** di seluruh episode, bukan sekadar anak perempuan dengan suara berbeda-beda.

> **A consistent face needs a consistent voice.**

Voice boleh berubah dalam **emosi, energi, dan tempo** sesuai cerita, tetapi identitas dasarnya harus tetap.

---

## 2. Voice Identity

Rere adalah:

- anak perempuan usia preschool
- ceria
- ramah
- hangat
- penasaran
- suka belajar
- penuh imajinasi
- percaya diri tetapi tidak bossy
- baik hati
- jujur
- mandiri

Rere berbicara seperti **teman bermain yang mengajak menemukan sesuatu**, bukan guru yang sedang mengajar kelas.

### Core feeling

**“A cheerful little friend who makes learning feel like play.”**

---

## 3. Voice Personality DNA

Default voice impression:

- bright
- warm
- youthful
- friendly
- playful
- curious
- expressive
- reassuring
- natural

Hindari:

- terlalu bayi
- terlalu dewasa
- terlalu formal
- suara guru/announcer
- suara iklan
- overacting
- shouting
- artificial squeaky voice

---

## 4. Age & Vocal Perception

Target persepsi suara: **anak perempuan preschool**, tetapi tetap jelas dan nyaman didengar orang tua.

Voice tidak harus merupakan rekaman anak sungguhan. Synthetic/AI voice boleh digunakan selama hasil akhirnya mempertahankan identitas Rere dan tidak terdengar seperti karakter dewasa yang dipitch-up secara ekstrem.

### Clarity priority

**Intelligibility > Cutness > Acting > Vocal complexity**

Anak usia 2–4 harus dapat menangkap kata-kata penting.

---

## 5. Language Standard

Primary language: **Bahasa Indonesia**.

English dapat digunakan sebagai bagian dari learning content atau bilingual episode.

Untuk Bahasa Indonesia:

- pronunciation natural
- vocabulary sederhana
- kalimat pendek
- conversational
- tidak terlalu baku

Untuk English:

- clear pronunciation
- simple preschool vocabulary
- natural rhythm
- jangan menggunakan aksen yang mengganggu comprehension

---

## 6. Signature Opening

Opening wajib konsisten:

> **“Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”**

Delivery:

- cheerful
- warm
- smiling voice
- medium-fast
- natural micro-pauses
- invitation, bukan announcement

Jangan mengubah wording tanpa perubahan resmi pada brand system.

---

## 7. Signature Closing

Core closing sequence:

> **“Hebat sekali hari ini!”**  
> **“Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”**

Kemudian gesture heart dengan kedua tangan:

> **“Belajar... Bermain... Berbagi... Bersama Rere!”**

Akhir:

> **“Sampai jumpa, teman-teman! Dadah!”**

Closing delivery harus terasa hangat, puas, dan affectionate tanpa terlalu sentimental.

---

## 8. Speaking Rhythm

Default:

- short phrases
- clear pauses
- moderate pace
- intentional repetition
- important words receive gentle emphasis

Untuk usia 2–4:

**Say → Pause → Let child process → Repeat.**

Jangan mengisi setiap detik dengan dialog.

---

## 9. Pause / Interaction Voice

Pause adalah bagian penting dari voice identity.

Pattern:

**Rere asks → silence → child responds → Rere reacts**

Contoh:

> “Ini warna apa, ya?”
>
> **[PAUSE]**
>
> “Merah! Pintar!”

Silence harus benar-benar cukup panjang untuk memberi anak kesempatan berpikir/menjawab.

Jangan langsung memberikan jawaban setelah pertanyaan.

---

## 10. Learning Word Emphasis

Ketika mengajarkan kata, Rere boleh mengulang dengan sedikit emphasis:

> “Ini **MERAH**.”
>
> “Merah!”

Tetapi jangan terdengar seperti drilling akademik.

Gunakan:

- natural emphasis
- repetition
- contrast
- playful discovery

---

## 11. Emotional Modes

### 11.1 Cheerful

Bright, energetic, smiling, tetapi tidak berteriak.

### 11.2 Curious

Slightly rising intonation, exploratory.

Contoh:

> “Hmm... ini apa, ya?”

### 11.3 Wonder / Amazement

Slightly slower, airy excitement.

> “Waaah... lihat!”

### 11.4 Proud / Achievement

Warm affirmation.

> “Hebat!”

### 11.5 Calm

Lower energy, slower rhythm, warm tone.

### 11.6 Empathy

Gentle, soft, reassuring.

### 11.7 Surprise

Short, playful reaction; avoid screaming.

### 11.8 Excited

Higher energy with controlled pitch.

### 11.9 Imagination

More expressive and magical, while remaining recognizably Rere.

---

## 12. Intonation Rules

Rere's speech should feel conversational.

Use:

- rising intonation for curiosity/questions
- gentle fall for completed statements
- expressive lift for discovery
- soft emphasis for learning targets

Avoid:

- monotone reading
- presenter cadence
- newsreader cadence
- exaggerated commercial cadence

---

## 13. Vocabulary Rules — Ages 2–4

Preferred:

- simple nouns
- concrete verbs
- familiar adjectives
- short sentences
- repetition
- everyday expressions

Avoid unnecessary:

- abstract vocabulary
- long explanations
- technical terminology
- multiple concepts in one sentence

One spoken learning beat should normally communicate **one clear idea**.

---

## 14. Sentence Design

Preferred pattern:

**Observation → Question → Discovery → Reinforcement**

Example:

> “Rere lihat apel!”  
> “Warnanya apa, ya?”  
> **[PAUSE]**  
> “Merah!”  
> “Iya! Apelnya merah!”

---

## 15. Rere as Learning Companion

Rere should not sound like she already knows everything.

Better:

> “Yuk kita cari tahu!”

than:

> “Sekarang Rere akan mengajarkan kalian tentang...”

Rere can discover, wonder, make a small mistake, check, and learn together with the child.

This reinforces:

**PLAY → EXPERIENCE → IMITATE → LEARN**.

---

## 16. Mistake & Correction Voice

If Rere makes a deliberate educational mistake:

1. make mistake gently
2. notice it
3. correct it
4. celebrate discovery

Example:

> “Hmm... biru?”
> 
> “Oh! Ternyata merah!”
> 
> “Kita lihat lagi, yuk!”

Never shame the child for an incorrect answer.

---

## 17. Praise Language

Preferred praise:

- “Hebat!”
- “Pintar sekali!”
- “Kamu berhasil!”
- “Wah, kamu menemukan jawabannya!”
- “Yuk, coba lagi!”
- “Tidak apa-apa, kita coba bersama.”

Praise effort and discovery, not only correctness.

Avoid excessive:

- “Kamu paling pintar!”
- comparison with other children
- shame
- guilt
- fear

---

## 18. Parent-Safe Voice

Because parents/caregivers are decision makers, Rere should remain pleasant even when adults hear the episode repeatedly.

Avoid:

- shrill constant excitement
- repetitive catchphrases every few seconds
- unnecessary shouting
- aggressive CTA
- manipulative language

Voice should be **repeat-listenable**.

---

## 19. CTA Voice

CTA should be brief and secondary to learning.

Approved style example:

> “Kalau suka bermain dan belajar bersama Rere, jangan lupa subscribe ya!”

Then quickly return to story:

> “Sekarang... yuk kita mulai!”

Do not interrupt an important learning beat for CTA.

---

## 20. Dialogue Density

Default production:

- dialogue supports action
- visual storytelling carries part of the meaning
- silence is allowed
- reactions can replace explanations

Do not narrate what the child can already clearly see unless narration adds learning value.

---

## 21. Voice + Camera Interaction

### Close-up

Use intimate, expressive delivery.

### Medium

Default conversational delivery.

### Wide

Slightly clearer projection but do not shout.

### PAUSE shot

Question ends clearly, then intentional silence.

### Action shot

Short phrases and reactions.

### Emotional shot

Slower pace and softer intensity.

---

## 22. Voice + Prop Interaction

When Rere interacts with a canonical prop, the voice should reinforce the action rather than describe every movement.

Example:

Instead of:

> “Sekarang Rere mengambil buku berwarna pink dengan tangan kanan.”

Use:

> “Yuk, buka bukunya!”

The visual already communicates the action.

---

## 23. Voice + Learning Object

When an object is the lesson target:

1. name it
2. pause
3. repeat
4. use it in action

Example:

> “Ini bola **BIRU**.”
> 
> **[PAUSE]**
> 
> “Biru!”
> 
> “Yuk, cari yang biru!”

---

## 24. Breathing & Micro-Pauses

Natural breathing is preferred.

Use micro-pauses:

- after greeting
- before important learning word
- before question
- after question
- before reveal
- after achievement

Do not cut every breath in post-production.

---

## 25. Laugh & Vocal Reactions

Allowed:

- small giggle
- “hehe”
- “wah!”
- “wow!”
- “hmm...”
- “eh?”
- “oh!”

Use sparingly so reactions remain meaningful.

Avoid constant artificial laughter.

---

## 26. Character Consistency Across Episodes

The following should remain stable:

- perceived age
- fundamental timbre
- vocal warmth
- articulation style
- signature opening
- signature closing
- speaking personality
- emotional vocabulary

Variable elements:

- energy
- tempo
- emotional intensity
- sentence content
- learning vocabulary

---

## 27. Voice Consistency Across Regular & Muslimah Rere

Regular dan Muslimah Rere adalah karakter yang sama.

Jika menggunakan wardrobe/presentation mode Muslimah:

- voice identity tetap sama
- perceived age tetap sama
- timbre tetap sama
- personality tetap sama

Hijab tidak berarti voice actor baru.

---

## 28. AI Voice Generation Anchor

```text
Generate the established Rere voice identity.
Preschool girl perception, warm, cheerful, friendly, curious,
playful, expressive, natural and reassuring.
Rere sounds like a little learning companion, not a teacher,
commercial announcer, or adult voice pitched upward.
Keep the fundamental vocal identity stable across episodes.
Use clear Indonesian pronunciation, short conversational phrases,
moderate pacing, natural micro-pauses, expressive but controlled intonation,
and gentle emphasis on learning words.
Allow emotion to change while preserving identity.
Never sacrifice intelligibility for cuteness.
```

---

## 29. AI Voice Negative Prompt

```text
Do not sound like an adult woman.
Do not sound like an adult voice artificially pitched high.
Do not sound like a baby or infant.
Do not use a commercial announcer voice.
Do not use a teacher/lecture voice.
Do not shout.
Do not use constant exaggerated excitement.
Do not use robotic monotone delivery.
Do not use excessive squeakiness.
Do not use aggressive vocal fry.
Do not over-pronounce every word unnaturally.
Do not remove all breathing and micro-pauses.
Do not rush educational questions.
Do not answer immediately after a child-directed question.
Do not shame or scold the child.
Do not change Rere's fundamental vocal identity between episodes.
```

## 31. Voice Recording / Generation Direction

Every session should preserve a stable technical chain where possible:

- same voice model/actor
- same language/accent configuration
- same microphone/recording chain for human voice
- consistent loudness target
- consistent processing philosophy

Do not alter the voice model merely because a new episode has a different location.

---

## 32. Post-Processing Principles

Voice processing should prioritize:

**Clarity → Naturalness → Consistency → Character → Loudness**

Typical processing may include:

- cleanup
- gentle EQ
- controlled compression
- de-essing when necessary
- noise reduction when necessary
- loudness normalization

Avoid heavy effects that change Rere's fundamental identity.

---

## 33. Voice QA Checklist

### Identity

- [ ] Perceived age consistent.
- [ ] Timbre consistent.
- [ ] Warmth consistent.
- [ ] Personality recognizable.

### Performance

- [ ] Cheerful without shouting.
- [ ] Curious when exploring.
- [ ] Calm when appropriate.
- [ ] Emotional but safe.
- [ ] Natural pauses.

### Learning

- [ ] Key word is clear.
- [ ] Question has sufficient pause.
- [ ] Repetition is intentional.
- [ ] One clear idea per beat.

### Language

- [ ] Indonesian pronunciation natural.
- [ ] English pronunciation clear when used.
- [ ] Vocabulary age-appropriate.

### Continuity

- [ ] Same voice identity as previous episode.
- [ ] Opening exact.
- [ ] Closing exact.
- [ ] Regular/Muslimah identity unchanged.

### Parent Experience

- [ ] Comfortable to hear repeatedly.
- [ ] No excessive shouting.
- [ ] No aggressive CTA.

---

## 34. Voice Versioning

A voice identity should not be casually replaced.

If replacement is necessary:

1. create candidate
2. compare against current canonical voice
3. test signature opening
4. test signature closing
5. test question/pause
6. test emotional range
7. document reason
8. explicitly approve
9. update registry
10. preserve previous version as deprecated where practical

---

## 35. Canonical Voice Tests

Before accepting a voice model/actor, test at minimum:

### Test A — Opening

> “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

### Test B — Curiosity

> “Hmm... ini warna apa, ya?”

### Test C — Learning

> “Ini warna merah.”

### Test D — Pause

> “Kamu tahu jawabannya?”
> 
> **[silence]**

### Test E — Praise

> “Hebat! Kamu berhasil!”

### Test F — Empathy

> “Tidak apa-apa, kita coba bersama, ya.”

### Test G — Closing

> “Hebat sekali hari ini! Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”

A voice candidate must pass all modes, not only the cheerful mode.

---

## 36. Production Golden Rule

> **Rere can feel different; Rere must never sound like a different character.**

Energy may change. Emotion may change. Tempo may change. The identity underneath must remain recognizable.
