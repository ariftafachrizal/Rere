# EP005 — RERE MENGENAL BAGIAN TUBUH

**Storyboard V01 — Production Lock Draft**  
**Target:** ages 2–4, 3–4 minutes  
**Pillar:** SPEAK  
**Learning promise:** mengenali dan menyebut mata, hidung, mulut, tangan, kaki serta meniru gerakan sederhana.

## Canonical Sources
Canonical Rere character + face/expression + regular outfit; canonical bedroom with safe child-scale mirror; body learning reference. No medical imagery.

## Shot Plan

### S01 — Opening | 0:00–0:12
Rere beside mirror. FS, child-height. Exact canonical opening.

### S02 — Mirror Game | 0:12–0:28
Rere notices reflection and says: “Rere mau bermain di depan cermin! Yuk kenali tubuh kita!”

### S03 — Eyes | 0:28–0:50
MCU/CU. Rere points gently to eyes: “Ini mata. Mana mata Rere?” PAUSE 4 sec. “Ini mata!” Eye direction must be clear.

### S04 — Nose | 0:50–1:12
Rere points to nose: “Ini hidung. Mana hidung?” PAUSE. “Ini hidung!”

### S05 — Mouth | 1:12–1:34
Rere points to mouth and smiles: “Ini mulut. Mana mulut?” PAUSE. “Ini mulut!”

### S06 — Hands | 1:34–1:58
MS with both hands visible. “Ini tangan. Yuk lambaikan tangan!” PAUSE 3 sec. “Dadah!”

### S07 — Feet | 1:58–2:22
MLS/full body. “Ini kaki. Yuk gerakkan kaki!” PAUSE 3 sec. Rere performs small safe movement.

### S08 — Recall | 2:22–3:10
MCU→MS. Rere points sequentially: mata, hidung, mulut, tangan, kaki. Child can answer along. No new vocabulary.

### S09 — Closing | 3:10–3:45
Canonical closing/end card.

## Gemini Constraints
Face reference is mandatory for face close-ups. Mirror reflection must be coherent. Reject malformed anatomy, extra fingers/limbs, adult proportions, distorted face, medical/clinical styling, frightening expressions, character drift, text artifacts and clutter.
