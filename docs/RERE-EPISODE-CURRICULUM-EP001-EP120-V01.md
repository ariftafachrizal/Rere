# RERE — EPISODE CURRICULUM EP001–EP120 V01

**Scope:** EP001–EP120  
**Purpose:** long-range content-control matrix for autonomous production  
**Status:** EP001–EP006 locked baseline; EP007–EP120 curriculum planning baseline  
**Rule:** this matrix controls learning progression; it does not replace the episode-specific script/storyboard lock.

## Production principles

- Target audience baseline: 2–4 years for the first production stage; adapt difficulty gradually rather than by age label alone.
- One primary learning promise per episode.
- Use PLAY → EXPERIENCE → IMITATE → LEARN.
- Interaction pattern: Rere → question/instruction → PAUSE → child response/action → Rere reinforcement.
- Repetition is intentional; avoid introducing multiple new concepts in one learning moment.
- Use concrete objects, gestures, movement and familiar environments before abstraction.
- Preserve canonical Rere identity, world, props, camera, lighting, voice and QA systems.
- No future episode is considered production-locked until its episode-specific script, storyboard, references, prompts and QA gate exist.

## Progression control

### EP001–EP006
Recognition, basic matching, counting 1–5, body vocabulary and one-step life routines.

### EP007–EP036
Comparison, spatial concepts, early patterns, counting expansion, familiar vocabulary and foundational social-emotional routines.

### EP037–EP060
Emotion vocabulary, communication, listening, routines, community awareness and simple safety behavior.

### EP061–EP084
Concrete nature/science exploration with safe observation and simple cause/effect.

### EP085–EP108
Early literacy, visual storytelling, drawing, pattern creation and simple problem solving.

### EP109–EP120
Retrieval, integration, transfer and celebration rather than introducing a large volume of new concepts.
