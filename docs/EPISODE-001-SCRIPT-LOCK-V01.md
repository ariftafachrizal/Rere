# RERE EPISODE 001 — SCRIPT LOCK V01

**Episode ID:** `RERE-EP-001`  
**Title:** Rere dan Petualangan Warna  
**Target:** 2–4 tahun  
**Language:** Bahasa Indonesia  
**Primary Pillar:** DISCOVER  
**Status:** SCRIPT LOCK V01  
**Date:** 2026-09-12

> This script is the production baseline. Dialogue timing may be adjusted during voice recording only when meaning, canonical opening/closing, learning objective, and participation structure remain intact.

## 1. Learning Promise

Anak diajak mengenali dan mulai menyebutkan lima warna melalui benda familiar:

**merah → kuning → biru → hijau → pink**

## 2. Production Rules

- Keep sentences short and concrete.
- One learning target at a time.
- Every color question has a deliberate PAUSE.
- Never shame a wrong answer.
- Rere is a companion, not a lecturer.
- Canonical opening and closing are exact.
- Optional English exposure is omitted from the spoken master script for clarity; it may be tested later as a controlled variant.

## 3. SCRIPT

### SCENE 01 — OPENING

**RERE** [warm smile, gentle wave]:

> “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

[SHORT BEAT]

### SCENE 02 — HOOK

**RERE** [looks at drawing book and crayons]:

> “Hari ini Rere mau menggambar!”

[looks at crayons]

> “Wah... ada banyak warna!”

[smiles]

> “Yuk, kita cari tahu!”

### SCENE 03 — CTA

**RERE** [brief, cheerful]:

> “Kalau suka main dan belajar bersama Rere, jangan lupa subscribe, ya!”

[SHORT BEAT]

> “Sekarang... yuk kita mulai!”

**Editorial note:** Keep this section very short. If pacing suffers, use a visual CTA instead and preserve the learning hook.

### SCENE 04 — MERAH

**RERE** [notices apple]:

> “Wah! Rere menemukan sesuatu!”

> “Ini apel.”

[looks at apple]

> “Warnanya apa, ya?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

**RERE** [warm confirmation]:

> “MERAH!”

> “Ayo bilang bersama Rere... me-rah!”

[gentle smile / reward cue]

### SCENE 05 — KUNING

**RERE** [notices banana]:

> “Ini pisang!”

> “Warnanya apa?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

**RERE:**

> “KUNING!”

> “Ku-ning!”

[gentle reward]

### SCENE 06 — BIRU

**RERE** [notices blue ball]:

> “Lihat! Ada bola!”

> “Bola ini warna...”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

**RERE:**

> “BIRU!”

[rolls ball gently]

> “Biru!”

### SCENE 07 — HIJAU

**RERE** [looks at green plant/leaf]:

> “Rere melihat tanaman!”

> “Daunnya warna apa?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

**RERE:**

> “HIJAU!”

> “Ayo bilang... hijau.”

[gentle smile]

### SCENE 08 — PINK

**RERE** [notices pink flower]:

> “Waaaah! Rere menemukan warna kesukaan Rere!”

> “Teman-teman tahu warna apa?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

**RERE:**

> “PINK!”

> “Rere suka pink!”

[Rere forms the signature two-hand heart]

### SCENE 09 — MINI QUIZ

**RERE:**

> “Yuk, kita coba lagi!”

#### Quiz 1

[Three familiar objects visible; red is clearly distinguishable.]

> “Mana yang warna MERAH?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

> “Betul!”

#### Quiz 2

[Three familiar objects visible; yellow is clearly distinguishable.]

> “Mana yang warna KUNING?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

> “Betul!”

#### Quiz 3

[Three familiar objects visible; pink is clearly distinguishable.]

> “Yang mana warna PINK?”

**[PAUSE — 3–5 seconds]**

> “Yeay! Betul!”

### SCENE 10 — CREATE + REVIEW

**RERE** [returns to drawing]:

> “Sekarang, yuk kita pakai warna-warna ini untuk menggambar!”

[draws with several colors]

> “Merah... kuning... biru... hijau... dan pink.”

[shows drawing]

> “Lihat! Gambar Rere sudah penuh warna!”

> “Ternyata warna ada di mana-mana!”

### SCENE 11 — ACHIEVEMENT

**RERE** [proud, warm]:

> “Hari ini kita sudah belajar warna merah, kuning, biru, hijau, dan pink.”

> “Hebat! Kamu sudah ikut mencari warna bersama Rere.”

### SCENE 12 — CANONICAL CLOSING

**RERE** [warm, slightly slower pace]:

> “Hebat sekali hari ini!”

> “Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”

[Rere forms heart with both hands]

> “Belajar... Bermain... Berbagi... Bersama Rere!”

> “Sampai jumpa, teman-teman! Dadah!”

**END CARD:**

> **BELAJAR • BERMAIN • BERBAGI**  
> **BERSAMA RERE!**

## 4. PAUSE MAP

| Moment | Purpose | Minimum guidance |
|---|---|---:|
| Merah | recognize/name | 3–5 sec |
| Kuning | recognize/name | 3–5 sec |
| Biru | recognize/name | 3–5 sec |
| Hijau | recognize/name | 3–5 sec |
| Pink | recognize/name | 3–5 sec |
| Quiz merah | choose | 3–5 sec |
| Quiz kuning | choose | 3–5 sec |
| Quiz pink | choose | 3–5 sec |

Final duration is determined after voice timing and child-centered edit review.

## 5. PERFORMANCE DIRECTION

Rere should sound:
- cheerful
- warm
- curious
- patient
- encouraging

Do not:
- shout color names
- make wrong answers feel negative
- rush through PAUSE
- overact every discovery
