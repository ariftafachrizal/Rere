# LIVE — Curriculum Pack

**Status:** Approved Working Curriculum — 2026-09-12

## Identity

**LIVE — Care for Myself, Others & My World**

## Purpose

LIVE membantu anak belajar hidup bersama orang lain, menjaga dirinya, dan semakin mampu melakukan hal-hal sederhana secara mandiri.

LIVE bukan curriculum kepatuhan. Anak belajar memahami **mengapa** suatu tindakan membantu dirinya, orang lain, atau lingkungan, lalu mempraktikkannya melalui rutinitas dan permainan.

## Core Areas

1. **Values & Respect** — kindness, respect, boundaries, care, responsibility toward others.
2. **Daily Life Skills & Independence** — dressing, eating routines, tidying, hygiene, organizing belongings, asking for help.
3. **Safety** — recognizing hazards, safe/unsafe choices, seeking trusted adult help, simple road/home/public safety.

Supporting capabilities:
- responsibility & contribution
- healthy habits & well-being
- agency and decision making
- persistence and flexibility
- cooperation

## Developmental Spine

> **NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY**

- **NOTICE:** see routines, boundaries, hazards, and needs.
- **UNDERSTAND:** connect action with simple reason/consequence.
- **PRACTICE:** try the behavior with modeling/support.
- **PARTICIPATE:** contribute to routines and shared responsibilities.
- **INDEPENDENTLY APPLY:** choose and use skills in familiar/new contexts with appropriate adult support.

## Fundamental Principles

### Meaning before obedience
Explain simple reasons: clean hands help keep food safe; helmets protect heads; putting toys away helps us find them.

### Model before demand
Rere demonstrates and invites participation rather than commanding from a distance.

### Safety is firm; other values can allow choice
Safety boundaries may be non-negotiable. Within safe boundaries, children can choose methods and preferences.

### Independence is scaffolded
The goal is increasing participation, not abandoning support too early.

### Practice in real contexts
Use routines, pretend play, stories, movement, and everyday problems.

### Respect body and boundaries
Children can learn to respect others while also having bodily autonomy and the ability to say no to non-safety interactions.

### Repetition with variation
The same life skill should recur in multiple contexts with gradually increasing independence.

## Content Pattern

**SITUATION → NOTICE → WHY IT MATTERS → MODEL → INVITE → PRACTICE → CHOOSE/CONTRIBUTE → REFLECT/CLOSE**

Not every episode needs every step literally.

## Core Area Guidance

### A. Values & Respect

Themes: kindness, respect, helping, listening, turn-taking, boundaries, caring for shared spaces, honesty in simple contexts, gratitude, and repairing harm.

Avoid reducing values to “good child vs bad child.” Focus on effects, relationships, and choices.

### B. Daily Life Skills & Independence

Themes: handwashing, brushing teeth, dressing, eating routines, carrying/putting belongings away, cleaning small messes, simple food preparation with adult supervision, asking for help, and completing small sequences.

Use **I do → We do → You try → You choose** as a flexible scaffold.

### C. Safety

Themes: road crossing, staying near trusted adults, hot/sharp objects, water safety, unknown adults, getting lost, household hazards, emergency help, and basic body safety.

Safety content should be concrete, calm, memorable, and action-oriented—not fear-based.

## LIVE + Other Pillars

Primary intention determines the pillar.

- LIVE + SPEAK: language used to perform a routine, ask for help, or communicate a safety need.
- LIVE + FEEL: frustration during routines, boundaries, cooperation, and emotional support.
- LIVE + DISCOVER: understanding cause/consequence of routines or safety conditions.
- LIVE + CREATE: pretend shop, cooking play, cleanup song, or role-play when creation/expression is primary.

## Quality Bar

An episode is ready when:

1. life/safety/value intention is clear;
2. the reason or meaning is understandable at the child's level;
3. child participation is real;
4. independence is scaffolded;
5. safety boundaries are unambiguous where needed;
6. no shame or fear is used to force behavior;
7. body autonomy and reasonable boundaries are respected;
8. routines are shown in concrete context;
9. repetition includes variation;
10. choices are safe and meaningful where possible;
11. support is available when a skill exceeds current ability;
12. language supports rather than replaces action;
13. supporting pillars remain subordinate;
14. success is defined by participation/progress, not perfect performance.

## Anti-Patterns

- obedience-only teaching;
- “good child/bad child” labels;
- threats or frightening consequences;
- shame for accidents or developmental dependence;
- forcing affection or physical contact;
- presenting every rule as equally non-negotiable;
- unrealistic independence expectations;
- safety lessons based on graphic danger;
- long lectures;
- repetitive quizzes instead of practice;
- assuming one routine works identically for every family/culture;
- making cleanliness perfection the goal.

## Three-Year Direction

### Year 1 — Participate in Everyday Life
NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE

Familiar routines, basic hygiene, simple safety, cleanup participation, asking for help, respectful interaction, and predictable sequences.

### Year 2 — Contribute with Purpose
PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE

Longer routine sequences, simple responsibility, shared spaces, turn-taking, safety choices, helping others, and beginning to explain why actions matter.

### Year 3 — Apply & Take Increasing Responsibility
PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY

More independent routines, simple planning, adapting when routines change, recognizing risks, contributing to group goals, and making safe/value-aligned choices.

## Parent Trust Layer

LIVE should reassure caregivers that Rere models practical, respectful, developmentally realistic behavior. Caregiver notes, when used, should be short and actionable.

LIVE is approved as a Core Pillar working framework and is ready for developmental progression and roadmap stress testing.
