# RERE — THUMBNAIL, TITLE & PACKAGING BIBLE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Production Standard  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Project:** YouTube Rere  
**Primary Audience:** Ages 2–4 (first 6 months)

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document defines how Rere episodes are packaged for discovery on YouTube while protecting the brand's educational, child-safe identity.

Packaging includes:
- title
- thumbnail
- episode naming
- visual hierarchy
- supporting text
- playlist/chapter naming
- description direction
- metadata principles
- consistency across episodes

Core principle:

> **Packaging should promise the experience honestly, then let the video deliver it.**

Do not optimize for clicks by misleading children or parents.

---

## 2. PACKAGING VALUES

Every title and thumbnail should reinforce:

**Value → Trust → Audience → Scale → Revenue**

Therefore:

1. Value comes first.
2. Trust must never be sacrificed for CTR.
3. Audience growth should come from clear positioning.
4. Scale comes from repeatable systems.
5. Revenue is downstream of a trusted brand.

---

## 3. TARGET VIEWER

Primary viewer:
- preschool child ages 2–4

Primary decision-maker / gatekeeper:
- parent or caregiver

Packaging therefore has two jobs:

### Child-facing
Communicate:
- fun
- recognizable character
- simple concept
- curiosity

### Parent-facing
Communicate:
- educational value
- safety
- clarity
- age appropriateness
- trustworthy presentation

The thumbnail may attract the child, but the overall packaging must earn the parent's trust.

---

## 4. BRAND POSITIONING

Rere is:

> **A cheerful preschool learning companion who invites children to play, discover, speak, feel, live, and create.**

Rere is not positioned as:
- a strict teacher
- a lecture channel
- a generic animation compilation
- a sensational children's entertainment channel
- a copied version of another children's brand

The packaging should make this distinction visible.

---

## 5. TITLE SYSTEM

Titles should be:
- clear
- searchable
- natural in Indonesian
- understandable to parents
- understandable at a glance
- specific about the learning/story topic
- consistent across episodes

Preferred structure:

> **Rere + Story/Activity + Learning Topic**

Examples:
- **Rere Belajar Warna | Petualangan Warna untuk Anak**
- **Rere Belajar Bentuk | Lingkaran, Kotak, dan Segitiga**
- **Rere Belajar Berhitung | Angka 1–5**
- **Rere di Taman | Belajar Mengenal Bunga dan Warna**

The exact title may vary according to search intent and episode story, but the Rere identity should remain recognizable.

---

## 6. TITLE LENGTH

Prefer titles that communicate the concept without unnecessary filler.

Do not stuff titles with:
- repeated keywords
- excessive emoji
- unrelated search terms
- competitor/channel names
- misleading claims
- exaggerated promises

A title should answer:

**“Apa yang akan anak pelajari atau alami?”**

---

## 7. TITLE FORMULA LIBRARY

### Learning-first
**Rere Belajar [TOPIK] | [CONTOH/KONSEP]**

### Story-first
**Rere dan [PETUALANGAN/KEGIATAN] | Belajar [TOPIK]**

### Activity-first
**Rere Bermain [AKTIVITAS] | Belajar [TOPIK]**

### World-first
**Rere di [TEMPAT] | Belajar [TOPIK]**

### Bilingual learning
**Rere Belajar [TOPIK] | [INDONESIAN] & [ENGLISH]**

Use bilingual titles only when the bilingual element is genuinely present in the episode.

---

## 8. TITLE LANGUAGE

Primary language: Bahasa Indonesia.

Use vocabulary parents naturally search for and children can recognize.

Prefer:
- warna
- bentuk
- angka
- berhitung
- hewan
- buah
- kendaraan
- anggota tubuh
- kebiasaan baik
- emosi
- berbagi
- kebersihan

Avoid unnecessarily academic terminology for preschool topics.

---

## 9. THUMBNAIL CORE PRINCIPLE

Thumbnail should communicate one primary idea in less than a second.

Preferred hierarchy:

**Rere → learning/story object → emotional cue → minimal text**

Do not attempt to show the entire episode in one thumbnail.

---

## 10. THUMBNAIL CHARACTER RULE

Rere should usually be the primary recognizable character.

Preferred:
- face clearly visible
- canonical hair
- canonical facial proportions
- canonical outfit appropriate to episode
- readable expression
- one clear gesture

Expressions should be:
- happy
- curious
- surprised
- proud
- excited but controlled

Avoid fear, distress, crying, or exaggerated panic as clickbait.

---

## 11. THUMBNAIL OBJECT RULE

Use one primary learning/story object where possible.

Examples:
- red crayon
- apple
- triangle block
- number cards
- flower
- bunny

The object must match the actual episode.

Never substitute a more attractive object that does not appear in the video merely to increase clicks.

---

## 12. THUMBNAIL COMPOSITION

Preferred structure:

**Rere + object + simple environment cue**

Use:
- clear foreground/background separation
- large readable shapes
- clean composition
- enough negative space
- strong focal hierarchy

Avoid:
- crowded collages
- many tiny objects
- visual noise
- random decorative elements
- excessive arrows/circles
- clickbait symbols

---

## 13. THUMBNAIL COLOR SYSTEM

Pink is Rere's signature accent, not the entire palette.

Use the established Rere world palette:
- soft pink
- cream
- warm white
- light wood
- pastel blue
- pastel green
- pastel yellow
- lavender
- controlled neutrals

The learning concept's actual colors must remain truthful.

For a colors episode, for example, do not make every object pink just to strengthen branding.

---

## 14. THUMBNAIL TEXT

Text is optional.

When used:
- keep it short
- use 1–4 words when possible
- prioritize the learning concept
- make it readable on a phone
- use consistent typography

Examples:
- **BELAJAR WARNA**
- **ANGKA 1–5**
- **KENAL BENTUK**
- **AYO BERBAGI!**

Do not repeat the entire YouTube title inside the thumbnail.

---

## 15. THUMBNAIL TYPOGRAPHY

Use a rounded, friendly, highly legible type style consistent with the Rere brand.

Requirements:
- high legibility
- strong size hierarchy
- simple letterforms
- sufficient contrast
- no overly decorative fonts

Typography should feel preschool-friendly without looking chaotic.

---

## 16. THUMBNAIL EMOTION

Emotion should communicate the episode's real emotional promise.

Examples:

| Episode | Suitable expression |
|---|---|
| Belajar Warna | curious / delighted |
| Belajar Bentuk | curious / excited |
| Berhitung | proud / playful |
| Berbagi | warm / happy |
| Emosi | empathetic / expressive |
| Nature | wonder / curiosity |

Do not manufacture extreme emotions unrelated to the story.

---

## 17. THUMBNAIL CAMERA LANGUAGE

Thumbnail imagery should inherit the Camera Bible.

Preferred:
- child-perspective feel
- clear focal point
- readable face
- simple depth
- strong silhouette

Close-up or medium-close compositions are often useful, but the learning object must remain recognizable.

---

## 18. THUMBNAIL LIGHTING

Thumbnail lighting should inherit the Lighting Bible.

Preferred:
- bright but soft
- warm/friendly
- clean separation
- clear eyes
- healthy skin tone
- controlled highlights

Avoid:
- dark horror-like contrast
- harsh rim lights
- extreme bloom
- neon overload
- artificial dramatic lighting unrelated to the episode

---

## 19. THUMBNAIL AI GENERATION ANCHOR

Use this anchor when generating a thumbnail:

> **Canonical Rere preschool thumbnail, soft pastel 3D CGI/kawaii, warm child-safe visual world, canonical Rere face and anatomy, canonical hairstyle and outfit, clear friendly expression, one primary learning/story object, simple readable composition, child-perspective visual language, soft lighting, pink as signature accent, high mobile readability, honest representation of the episode, clean background, emotionally warm, educational and trustworthy.**

### Negative prompt

Avoid:
- identity drift
- different face
- different hairstyle
- adult-looking child
- exaggerated anatomy
- horror
- fear
- crying clickbait
- aggressive expressions
- crowded composition
- excessive text
- misleading objects
- fake episode elements
- random logos
- competitor branding
- copyrighted character imitation
- photorealistic child appearance
- unsafe imagery
- flashing/strobe visual language

---

## 20. THUMBNAIL VARIANT SYSTEM

Create variants deliberately rather than regenerating randomly.

Recommended A/B variants:

### Variant A — Character-led
Rere face + object.

### Variant B — Interaction-led
Rere performing the activity.

### Variant C — Learning-led
Rere + multiple examples of the core concept, but still visually simple.

Keep canonical identity unchanged across variants.

---

## 21. A/B TESTING PRINCIPLE

Testing should evaluate packaging clarity, not encourage deception.

Possible variables:
- expression
- object scale
- text presence
- text wording
- crop
- background simplicity

Do not simultaneously change every variable if the goal is to learn what works.

The winning thumbnail should still satisfy all brand and child-safety rules.

---

## 22. DESCRIPTION SYSTEM

Descriptions should help parents understand the video.

Recommended structure:

1. One-sentence episode summary
2. Learning objective
3. What children practice
4. Short activity invitation
5. Relevant playlist/channel context
6. Appropriate disclosures/credits where needed

Example direction:

> “Yuk bermain bersama Rere sambil mengenal warna merah, kuning, biru, hijau, dan pink. Anak diajak mengamati, menyebutkan, membandingkan, dan mencoba mengenali warna melalui permainan sederhana.”

Do not keyword-stuff the description.

---

## 23. EPISODE METADATA

Each episode should maintain structured internal metadata:

- episode ID
- title
- learning pillar(s)
- target age
- learning objective
- key vocabulary
- world/location
- primary props
- canonical character/outfit
- thumbnail version
- title version
- production version

Suggested episode ID:

`RERE-EP-001`

---

## 24. PLAYLIST SYSTEM

Playlists should organize learning journeys rather than merely maximize video count.

Initial playlist concepts:

- **Rere Belajar Warna**
- **Rere Belajar Bentuk**
- **Rere Belajar Angka**
- **Rere Belajar Bahasa**
- **Rere Mengenal Emosi**
- **Rere dan Kebiasaan Baik**
- **Rere Menjelajah Alam**
- **Rere Bermain & Berkreasi**

Playlist names should remain simple and parent-friendly.

---

## 25. EPISODE NAMING CONSISTENCY

Internal canonical naming:

`RERE-EP-[NUMBER]-[SHORT-SLUG]`

Example:

`RERE-EP-001-BELAJAR-WARNA`

Public title may be optimized for readability, but internal IDs must remain stable.

---

## 26. SEARCH / DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE

Search optimization should describe the actual content.

Use relevant terms naturally in:
- title
- first part of description
- playlist
- spoken content where natural
- metadata where appropriate

Do not create irrelevant metadata around trending adult topics or unrelated high-volume keywords.

---

## 27. PARENT TRUST SIGNALS

Packaging should make the channel feel:
- intentional
- clean
- safe
- educational
- consistent
- professionally produced

Trust is strengthened by consistency between:
- title promise
- thumbnail promise
- actual episode
- age target
- visual quality
- learning objective

---

## 28. CHILD-SAFE PACKAGING

Never use packaging that relies on:
- fear
- injury
- humiliation
- danger as bait
- sexualized imagery
- disturbing facial expressions
- fake emergencies
- manipulative urgency

The thumbnail should make a child curious, not anxious.

---

## 29. NO MISLEADING PACKAGING

Never:
- put a character in the thumbnail who does not appear in the episode
- promise a lesson that is absent
- imply a dramatic event that does not happen
- use unrelated popular characters
- use fake “secret” or “shocking” claims
- exaggerate educational outcomes

The thumbnail and title are a contract with the viewer.

---

## 30. BRAND CONSISTENCY

Across the channel, maintain:
- Rere's canonical face
- canonical hair
- recognizable pink accent
- soft 3D style
- warm lighting language
- rounded forms
- restrained composition
- consistent typography
- consistent title structure

A viewer should recognize a Rere thumbnail before reading the channel name.

---

## 31. REGULAR & MUSLIMAH RERE

Both versions belong to the same brand universe.

Packaging may show either presentation when appropriate, but both must preserve:
- canonical Rere identity
- face
- proportions
- visual style
- color language
- emotional language

Muslimah styling must remain respectful and age-appropriate.

---

## 32. CONTENT PROMISE

Every package should communicate at least one of:

**Discover something → try something → learn something → feel something → create something.**

This maps to the five pillars:

- DISCOVER
- SPEAK
- FEEL
- LIVE
- CREATE

PLAY remains the method, not a separate pillar.

---

## 33. SHORT-FORM / VERTICAL PACKAGING

If Rere content is later adapted for Shorts or other vertical platforms:

Preserve:
- canonical identity
- learning promise
- child-safe emotion
- simple focal point
- readable framing

Do not turn Rere into a high-intensity meme/editing identity merely because the platform is short-form.

Short-form packaging may be faster, but the Rere brand language remains stable.

---

## 34. CROSS-PLATFORM RULE

Platform adaptation changes:
- aspect ratio
- crop
- title length
- text placement
- delivery format

It must not change:
- Rere identity
- learning philosophy
- trust standard
- child-safety standard
- canonical visual language

---

## 35. PACKAGING QA — TITLE

- [ ] Rere identity is recognizable
- [ ] learning/story topic is clear
- [ ] wording is natural
- [ ] no keyword stuffing
- [ ] no misleading claim
- [ ] appropriate for parents
- [ ] appropriate for preschool content

---

## 36. PACKAGING QA — THUMBNAIL

- [ ] Rere canonical asset used
- [ ] correct outfit
- [ ] correct expression
- [ ] one clear focal point
- [ ] learning/story object is real to the episode
- [ ] mobile readability checked
- [ ] text minimal if used
- [ ] no visual clutter
- [ ] no clickbait fear
- [ ] color remains truthful

---

## 37. PACKAGING QA — EPISODE MATCH

Before publishing:

**Title ↔ Thumbnail ↔ First 30 Seconds ↔ Actual Lesson**

must tell the same basic story.

A viewer should never feel that the package promised one video and delivered another.

---

## 38. PACKAGING EXPERIMENT LOG

For each test, record:

- date
- episode ID
- title version
- thumbnail version
- hypothesis
- change made
- observation
- result
- decision

Do not treat one video's result as a universal rule.

Build a body of evidence across multiple episodes.

---

## 39. RECOMMENDED FIRST 10 EPISODE PACKAGING TESTS

For the first 10 episodes, deliberately test:

1. character-led vs object-led thumbnail
2. text vs no text
3. close-up vs medium shot
4. learning-first title vs story-first title
5. one object vs two related objects
6. curious vs delighted expression
7. simple background vs environment cue
8. short title vs descriptive title
9. color-led composition vs character-led composition
10. Indonesian-only vs genuine bilingual title where appropriate

Keep the tests controlled and document results.

---

## 40. THUMBNAIL GENERATION WORKFLOW

**Episode objective → choose focal concept → select canonical Rere asset → select canonical prop → select canonical world cue → compose → render → mobile QA → approve → version.**

Never begin from a random generated Rere image when a canonical asset exists.

---

## 41. TITLE GENERATION WORKFLOW

**Episode objective → identify parent-searchable concept → identify child-friendly wording → choose title formula → draft variants → check truthfulness → QA → publish.**

---

## 42. PACKAGING LOCK

Once approved:

- title version is locked for the current release
- thumbnail version is locked
- description structure is locked
- internal episode metadata is locked

Later optimization should create a new version rather than silently overwriting historical decisions.

---

## 43. AI PACKAGING PRINCIPLE

AI may assist with:
- title ideation
- thumbnail concepts
- copy variants
- metadata organization
- A/B test hypotheses

AI must not independently redefine:
- Rere identity
- educational positioning
- child-safety standard
- truthfulness of the package

Human review remains required.

---

## 44. FINAL PACKAGING PRINCIPLE

> **Make the promise clear. Make the promise honest. Make the promise recognizable as Rere.**

A successful Rere package should communicate:

**“Di sini ada Rere. Anak bisa bermain. Anak bisa ikut mencoba. Dan ada sesuatu yang bermakna untuk dipelajari.”**
