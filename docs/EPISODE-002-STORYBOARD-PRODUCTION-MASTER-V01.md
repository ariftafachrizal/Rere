# RERE EPISODE 002 — STORYBOARD PRODUCTION MASTER V01

**Episode ID:** `RERE-EP-002`  
**Working Title:** **Rere dan Petualangan Bentuk**  
**Target Age:** 2–4 tahun  
**Format:** 16:9 landscape  
**Target Duration:** 3:00–4:00  
**Primary Pillar:** DISCOVER  
**Secondary Pillars:** SPEAK, CREATE  
**Status:** PRE-PRODUCTION STORYBOARD MASTER V01

## 1. Purpose
Blueprint visual dan temporal untuk Gemini/editing. Gemini renders; human review decides acceptance.

## 2. Canonical Locks
- Character: `RERE-CHAR-01`, exact canonical face/anatomy/outfit/pony tail.
- World: `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`, unchanged architecture and geography.
- Props: use canonical Learning Book/Crayon Kit only where required.
- Learning reference: canonical Shapes Learning Sheet.
- No shot may create a new canonical design.

## 3. Visual Experience
The child should feel Rere is genuinely discovering shapes through play. Learning priority: **Rere → shape → action → child response**.

## 4. Learning Object Lock
Three and only three target shapes:
- **Lingkaran:** closed round shape, clean silhouette.
- **Persegi:** four visually equal sides, four corners.
- **Segitiga:** three sides, three corners.

Use one consistent geometry, scale relationship and material treatment throughout the episode. Avoid visually confusing decorative patterns.

## 5. Shot-by-Shot Production Storyboard

### S01 — SIGNATURE OPENING
**Time:** 0:00–0:12 | **Framing:** FS→MCU | **Camera:** `CAM-RERE-INTRO-01` | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** familiar entry and invitation.

**Action:** Rere faces camera, smiles, waves, self-points, opens hands invitingly.

**Dialogue:** canonical opening: “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

**Audio:** canonical opening sonic identity.

**Reject:** identity drift, adult proportions, theatrical presentation, extra fingers/limbs.

### S02 — SHAPE MISSION
**Time:** 0:12–0:28 | **Framing:** WS→MS | **Camera:** `CAM-RERE-PLAYROOM-ESTABLISH-01` | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** establish three shapes as a playful discovery.

**Action chain:** Rere moves to learning area → opens activity area → notices three shape pieces → reacts with curiosity.

**Dialogue:** “Hari ini Rere mau bermain dengan bentuk! Wah, ada beberapa bentuk. Yuk, kita lihat!”

**Composition:** all three shapes visible but not overlapping; each has clear silhouette.

**Reject:** extra shape types, unreadable geometry, clutter, random symbols/text.

### S03 — CIRCLE
**Time:** 0:28–0:48 | **Framing:** MS→CU→MCU | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** recognize lingkaran and invite pointing.

**Action:** Rere picks up/displays circle → traces its round edge → asks child to point → 4 sec pause → reveals/reinforces.

**Dialogue:** “Ini bentuk lingkaran. Bulat, ya! Bisa tunjuk bentuk lingkaran?” **PAUSE 4 sec** “Iya! Lingkaran! Lin-gka-ran.”

**Critical rule:** during question/pause, the target must be visually clear but Rere should not perform an answer-giving gesture that makes the response automatic.

**Reject:** oval/circle drift, multiple plausible circles, hidden target, geometry deformation.

### S04 — SQUARE
**Time:** 0:48–1:08 | **Framing:** MS→CU→MCU | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** recognize persegi.

**Action:** transition from circle → display square → trace/indicate four sides → question → 4 sec pause → reveal.

**Dialogue:** “Ini bentuk persegi. Ada empat sisi. Mana bentuk persegi?” **PAUSE 4 sec** “Ini persegi! Per-se-gi.”

**Critical rule:** square must remain a true square, not rectangle/diamond.

**Reject:** unequal sides, perspective making geometry ambiguous, multiple candidate squares.

### S05 — TRIANGLE
**Time:** 1:08–1:28 | **Framing:** MS→CU→MCU | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** recognize segitiga.

**Action:** display triangle → Rere points/traces three corners/sides → question → 4 sec pause → reveal.

**Dialogue:** “Ini bentuk segitiga. Ada tiga sisi. Mana segitiga?” **PAUSE 4 sec** “Betul! Segitiga! Se-gi-ti-ga.”

**Reject:** four-sided shapes, rounded triangle, multiple plausible triangles, geometry drift.

### S06 — MATCHING
**Time:** 1:28–1:55 | **Framing:** WS→top-ish learning CU | **Camera:** `CAM-LEARN-MATCH-01` | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** transfer recognition into matching.

**Action:** three source shapes and three identical destination shapes arranged with obvious one-to-one matches. Rere completes circle→circle, square→square, triangle→triangle.

**Dialogue:** “Sekarang kita cocokkan! Lingkaran dengan lingkaran, persegi dengan persegi, segitiga dengan segitiga.”

**Critical rule:** only one obvious match should be possible for each move; do not introduce color as a competing matching criterion.

**Reject:** two correct answers, changing geometry, occlusion, ambiguous layout.

### S07 — CREATE
**Time:** 1:55–2:35 | **Framing:** MS, slight top angle | **Motion:** 1–2

**Purpose:** reinforce shape vocabulary through creation.

**Action:** Rere uses learned shapes to build one simple picture (e.g. house: square body + triangle roof + circle window). Keep the three shape identities visibly intact.

**Dialogue:** “Sekarang Rere mau membuat gambar dari bentuk-bentuk ini. Yuk, kita susun!”

**Reject:** new shape types, complex puzzle, shapes morphing, object count becoming confusing.

### S08 — REVIEW
**Time:** 2:35–3:05 | **Framing:** MCU | **Motion:** 0–1

**Purpose:** recall only.

**Action:** three shapes displayed one at a time in stable order. Rere asks “Ini apa?” and waits briefly, then names each.

**Dialogue:** “Ayo ingat lagi. Ini apa? Lingkaran. Ini apa? Persegi. Ini apa? Segitiga.”

**Reject:** new concept, speed too fast for response, shape redesign.

### S09 — SIGNATURE CLOSING
**Time:** 3:05–3:40 | **Framing:** FS centered | **Camera:** `CAM-RERE-CLOSING-01` | **Motion:** 1

**Action/dialogue:** exact canonical closing with heart gesture and end card.

**Reject:** changed wording, blocked face/hands, oversized end card.

## 6. Continuity Map
Same playroom, same daytime lighting, same Rere outfit, stable screen geography. Shape geometry, material and scale relationship must remain consistent. Maintain object state between adjacent shots where practical.

## 7. Camera / Lighting
Child-height perspective, gentle movement, no dutch angles, no rapid zooms, no handheld shake. Soft warm daylight, diffused key, gentle fill, soft contact shadows, stable exposure and accurate shape readability.

## 8. Episode Acceptance Gate
All 9 shots must pass canonical identity, learning-object geometry, learning clarity, continuity, camera, lighting, motion and safety QA before voice/edit assembly.
