## 1. PURPOSE

This document turns the Rere production system into the concrete production plan for Episode 001.

The episode is the first practical validation of the canonical Rere production pipeline.

Core learning philosophy:

> **PLAY → EXPERIENCE → IMITATE → LEARN**

Core episode pattern:

> **Story → Discovery → Question → PAUSE → Answer → Reward → Repeat**

---

## 2. LEARNING OBJECTIVE

By the end of the episode, a child should have opportunities to recognize and name:

- merah
- kuning
- biru
- hijau
- pink

using familiar objects in Rere's playroom.

### Observable child actions

The child may:
- look
- point
- choose
- name
- match
- repeat

The episode does not require the child to master all five colors in one viewing.

---

## 3. CONTENT PROMISE

> **Rere mengajak anak bermain sambil menemukan dan mengenal warna melalui benda-benda yang familiar.**

The story gives the learning a reason to happen: Rere wants to make a colorful picture and needs to find colors around her.

Rere greets the audience.

# 8. SCENE PLAN

## SCENE 01 — CANONICAL OPENING

**Purpose:** Establish Rere identity and invite participation.

**Location:** Playroom

**Camera:** Friendly medium/full-body presentation.

**Action:** Rere faces camera and greets audience.

**Dialogue:**

> “Hai teman-teman, aku Rere, main dan belajar bersama Rere, yuk!”

**Audio:** Canonical opening identity.

**Motion:** Gentle wave, warm smile.

**Duration:** ~8–12 sec including visual opening identity.

**QA:** Exact opening line must remain unchanged.

---

## SCENE 02 — HOOK: RERE MAU MENGGAMBAR

**Purpose:** Create story motivation.

**Visual:** Rere sits/stands at a child-scale activity area with paper and Crayon Kit.

**Action:** Rere opens/looks at crayons.

**Dialogue direction:**

> “Rere mau menggambar. Wah, krayonnya banyak sekali!”

Then:

> “Rere mau cari warna-warna yang cantik. Yuk!”

**Camera:** Medium → gentle focus on crayons.

**Audio:** Light discovery music; soft interaction SFX.

**Duration:** ~15–20 sec.

---

## SCENE 03 — MERAH

**Learning target:** merah

**Visual:** Rere notices a clearly red object in the playroom and/or red crayon.

**Dialogue:**

> “Hmm... ini warna apa?”

**PAUSE:** 3–5 sec.

**Answer:**

> “Merah! Ini warna merah.”

**Child invitation:**

> “Coba bilang... merah.”

**Reward:** Rere smiles; subtle reward cue.

**Duration:** ~20–25 sec.

**Editing:** Keep red object visible throughout PAUSE.

---

## SCENE 04 — KUNING

**Learning target:** kuning

**Visual:** Rere finds a yellow object/crayon.

**Dialogue:**

> “Sekarang, yang ini warna apa?”

**PAUSE:** 3–5 sec.

**Answer:**

> “Kuning! Ini warna kuning.”

> “Yuk, bilang... kuning.”

**Duration:** ~20–25 sec.

---

## SCENE 05 — BIRU

**Learning target:** biru

**Visual:** Rere finds a blue object/crayon.

**Dialogue:**

> “Lihat! Warna apa, ya?”

**PAUSE:** 3–5 sec.

**Answer:**

> “Biru! Ini warna biru.”

> “Hebat, coba lagi... biru.”

**Duration:** ~20–25 sec.

---

## SCENE 06 — HIJAU

**Learning target:** hijau

**Visual:** Rere finds a green object, preferably visually distinct from the previous examples.

**Dialogue:**

> “Nah, kalau yang ini?”

**PAUSE:** 3–5 sec.

**Answer:**

> “Hijau! Ini warna hijau.”

> “Ayo bilang... hijau.”

**Duration:** ~20–25 sec.

---

## SCENE 07 — PINK

**Learning target:** pink

**Visual:** Rere finds a pink object. Use pink as a natural signature color, not as an excuse to recolor unrelated props.

**Dialogue:**

> “Wah, ini warna favorit Rere. Warna apa?”

**PAUSE:** 3–5 sec.

**Answer:**

> “Pink! Ini warna pink.”

> “Pink!”

**Duration:** ~20–25 sec.

---

## SCENE 08 — MINI MATCH / REVIEW

**Purpose:** Reinforce recognition.

Show 2–3 familiar color examples.

**Dialogue direction:**

> “Yuk kita ingat lagi.”

> “Mana warna merah?”

**PAUSE**

> “Merah!”

Repeat with selected colors.

Do not attempt to test all five if runtime becomes rushed.

**Duration:** ~25–35 sec.

---

## SCENE 09 — CREATE: GAMBAR RERE

**Purpose:** Connect recognition to creation.

**Visual:** Rere uses the crayons to make a simple colorful picture.

**Action:** Choose colors, draw, show picture.

**Dialogue direction:**

> “Sekarang, yuk kita pakai warna-warna ini untuk menggambar!”

Then name selected colors naturally while drawing.

**Learning:** CREATE supports the main DISCOVER objective.

**Duration:** ~25–35 sec.

---

## SCENE 10 — REVIEW + ACHIEVEMENT

**Purpose:** Close the learning loop.

Rere shows the colorful drawing.

Dialogue direction:

> “Hari ini kita sudah belajar warna merah, kuning, biru, hijau, dan pink.”

> “Hebat! Kamu sudah ikut mencari warna bersama Rere.”

**Duration:** ~15–20 sec.

---

## SCENE 11 — CANONICAL CLOSING

Use exact established sequence:

- “Hebat sekali hari ini!”
- “Terima kasih sudah belajar bersama Rere!”
- both hands form heart
- “Belajar... Bermain... Berbagi... Bersama Rere!”
- “Sampai jumpa, teman-teman! Dadah!”

End card:

> **BELAJAR • BERMAIN • BERBAGI**  
> **BERSAMA RERE!**

**Duration:** ~15–20 sec.

# 13. AUDIO MAP

### Global
- canonical Rere voice
- playroom ambience
- restrained music
- canonical sonic identity

### Learning scenes
Use:

**Voice > learning SFX > music > ambience**

---

# 14. EDITING MAP

Use:

**Establish → Discover → Focus → Question → PAUSE → Answer → Reward → Progress**

For each color:

1. show Rere
2. reveal/point to object
3. name question
4. PAUSE
5. answer
6. child imitation invitation
7. reward
8. move on

Avoid rapid color montage before the child has learned the individual colors.

# 16. COLOR TEACHING RULE

The five target colors must be visually distinguishable.

When introducing a target color:
- use a clear representative object
- avoid visually similar competing colors
- keep background supportive
- ensure lighting does not distort the target color
- name the color clearly

For the pink scene, pink may also reinforce Rere's signature identity, but the educational object must remain clear.

---

# 17. REVIEW DESIGN

Review should use familiar objects rather than introducing entirely new ones.

Suggested sequence:

**See → question → PAUSE → answer → repeat.**

Use 2–3 examples if runtime is limited.

---

# 18. CREATE DESIGN

The drawing activity connects recognition to action.

Rere should visibly:

**choose color → use color → show result**

This creates a concrete bridge between:

**DISCOVER → CREATE**

The picture does not need to be artistically complex.

The learning value is in the child's recognition and use of colors.

---

# 19. CHILD PARTICIPATION CHECK

The episode should contain at least five clear participation opportunities, one per target color.

Each should answer:

> **“Sekarang giliran anak.”**

The child should not need to respond perfectly for the episode to remain positive.

---

# 20. SAFETY / DEVELOPMENT CHECK

Avoid:
- scary events
- dangerous objects
- unsafe imitation
- rapid flashing
- loud surprise effects
- shame for wrong answers
- competitive pressure

Rere should respond positively to attempts.

# 24. PRODUCTION NOTES

Episode 001 is deliberately simple.

Its job is not to demonstrate every capability of Rere.

Its job is to validate whether the system can reliably produce:

- stable Rere
- stable world
- stable props
- clear learning
- meaningful child participation
- coherent audio
- appropriate pacing
- honest packaging

Any production problem discovered here should be converted into a system improvement before scaling to more episodes.

---

# 25. SUCCESS CRITERIA

Episode 001 is successful when:

1. Rere is instantly recognizable.
2. The five target colors are visually and verbally clear.
3. The child receives repeated opportunities to respond.
4. The story gives a natural reason to learn colors.
5. The video feels playful rather than instructional-heavy.
6. The pace is calm enough to process.
7. The final creation gives the episode a satisfying payoff.
8. The title and thumbnail honestly represent the experience.
9. The production team can reproduce the process for Episode 002.

---

# 26. FINAL PRINCIPLE

> **Episode 001 is the prototype of the Rere production machine, not merely the first video.**

If the pipeline works here, document the lessons, improve the system, and reuse it.

If something fails, fix the system before scaling.

The objective is:

**Episode 001 → validated workflow → Episode 002–010 → repeatable production system → scalable Rere library.**
