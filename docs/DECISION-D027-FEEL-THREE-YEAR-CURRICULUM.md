# D-027 — FEEL Three-Year Curriculum

**Status:** Approved — 2026-09-12

## Decision

FEEL is formally established as the third Core Pillar of Rere's five-pillar learning architecture, with the identity:

> **FEEL — Understand Myself & Others**

Its working developmental spine is:

> **NOTICE → NAME → REGULATE WITH SUPPORT → INTERACT → CARE → BEGIN TO CHOOSE**

## Three-Year Roadmap

- **Year 1 — Recognize & Feel Safe:** NOTICE → NAME → REGULATE WITH SUPPORT.
- **Year 2 — Practice Together:** REGULATE WITH SUPPORT → INTERACT → CARE.
- **Year 3 — Choose, Repair & Collaborate:** INTERACT → CARE → BEGIN TO CHOOSE.

## Core Areas

1. Emotional Recognition & Naming
2. Self-Regulation & Coping
3. Social Interaction
4. Empathy & Perspective-Taking Beginnings

Supporting skills remain cross-cutting: self-awareness, confidence, frustration tolerance, flexibility, conflict resolution basics, agency, asking for help, and relationship repair.

## Key Principles

- Emotion is valid; behavior has boundaries.
- Co-regulation before self-regulation.
- Recognition before control.
- Model, don't moralize.
- Practice through play and story.
- Repetition with variation.
- Agency and safety over obedience.

## Stress-Test Result

A 40-case stress test covering emotional recognition, regulation, social interaction, empathy, conflict, boundaries, multilingual participation, nonverbal response, storytelling, music, pretend play, and cross-pillar boundaries produced **PASS** with no structural changes required.

## Consequence

FEEL is ready to enter the broader Rere three-year curriculum architecture as a stable learning map. Detailed episodes remain flexible and must continue to follow the Primary Learning Intention rule.
