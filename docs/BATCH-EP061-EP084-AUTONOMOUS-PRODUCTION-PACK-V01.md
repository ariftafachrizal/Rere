### Safety/QA
No unsafe ingestion, chemicals, sharp tools, heat exposure, deep water or direct insect handling. EP068 uses safe surface-level temperature concepts only. EP071 uses oversized child-safe magnet props and no swallowing hazard. EP073 adult-supervised bubble activity. EP083 explicitly observes insects from distance. EP084 uses safe, clean, non-hazardous litter or simulated litter. No scientific claim beyond visible observation. Every experiment has one clear observable outcome.
