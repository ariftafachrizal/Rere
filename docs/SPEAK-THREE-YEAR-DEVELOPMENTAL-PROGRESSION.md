# Rere — SPEAK Three-Year Developmental Progression

**Status:** Approved Working Framework — 2026-09-12

## Purpose

Dokumen ini menerjemahkan SPEAK dari framework pillar menjadi **peta perkembangan tiga tahun**. Peta ini bukan checklist umur dan bukan assessment formal. Fungsinya adalah membantu Rere menentukan bagaimana pengalaman komunikasi dapat berkembang dari partisipasi awal menuju komunikasi yang semakin mandiri dan fleksibel.

> **Stable progression, flexible experience.**

Usia adalah developmental reference, bukan gate. Anak dapat berada pada level berbeda untuk listening, expressive language, social communication, narrative language, dan English exposure.

---

# 1. Core Developmental Progression

**NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → EXPRESS → INTERACT → COMMUNICATE**

Perkembangan tidak selalu linear. Anak dapat maju, mengulang, atau menunjukkan beberapa fase sekaligus tergantung konteks, bahasa, familiaritas, dan dukungan orang dewasa.

| Phase | Reference age* | Dominant growth | Typical child experience |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | ±2–2.5 | NOTICE | mendengar, memperhatikan, mengenali |
| 2 | ±2.5–3 | UNDERSTAND | mengikuti, memilih, menunjukkan pemahaman |
| 3 | ±3–3.5 | RESPOND | menunjuk, bertindak, menjawab, meniru |
| 4 | ±3.5–4 | EXPRESS | meminta, menyebut, menggambarkan, menyampaikan pengalaman |
| 5 | ±4–4.5 | INTERACT | bergiliran, bertanya, menjawab, mempertahankan percakapan singkat |
| 6 | ±4.5–5 | COMMUNICATE | berbagi ide, bercerita, menjelaskan, memecahkan masalah bersama |

\*Reference age mengikuti kebutuhan roadmap Rere dan tidak dimaksudkan sebagai milestone klinis.

# 2. Phase-by-Phase Standard

## Phase 1 — NOTICE

**Child question:** “What is that sound/word?”

### Developmental focus
- memperhatikan suara dan ucapan;
- mengenali kata yang sering muncul;
- menghubungkan kata dengan benda, tindakan, orang, atau ekspresi;
- menikmati lagu, rhyme, dan pola bunyi;
- mengenali nama diri dan benda familiar.

### Rere experience standard
- kata dan frasa konsisten;
- visual, gesture, action, dan ekspresi memperkuat makna;
- repetition tinggi tetapi ringan;
- tidak menuntut verbal production.

### Evidence of participation
Anak memperhatikan, melihat, tersenyum, bergerak, menoleh, memilih, atau menunjukkan recognition.

---

## Phase 2 — UNDERSTAND

**Child question:** “What does it mean?”

### Developmental focus
- mengikuti instruksi sederhana;
- memahami pertanyaan familiar;
- memilih objek berdasarkan kata/deskripsi;
- memahami kejadian sederhana dalam cerita;
- menunjukkan pemahaman tanpa harus berbicara.

### Rere experience standard
- meaning selalu tersedia melalui context;
- satu instruksi/pertanyaan memiliki tujuan jelas;
- respons nonverbal diperlakukan sebagai valid;
- complexity naik secara bertahap.

### Evidence of participation
Anak melakukan tindakan yang sesuai, menunjuk pilihan, mengambil benda, atau mengantisipasi kejadian.

---

## Phase 3 — RESPOND

**Child question:** “How can I answer?”

### Developmental focus
Respons dapat berupa:
- gesture;
- tindakan;
- memilih;
- suara;
- satu kata;
- home language;
- English;
- kombinasi bahasa;
- frasa pendek.

### Rere experience standard
- memberi processing time;
- menyediakan pilihan response;
- tidak memaksakan kalimat lengkap;
- menerima approximate production;
- model diberikan setelah atau bersama respons anak.

### Evidence of participation
Anak mengambil giliran dan memberikan respons bermakna, walaupun belum verbal atau belum dalam English.

---

## Phase 4 — EXPRESS

**Child question:** “How can I say what I mean?”

### Developmental focus
Anak mulai menggunakan bahasa untuk:
- meminta;
- menolak;
- memilih;
- menamai;
- mendeskripsikan;
- menyatakan preference;
- meminta bantuan;
- memberi informasi;
- menceritakan pengalaman sederhana.

### Rere experience standard
- language function lebih penting daripada grammar perfection;
- vocabulary dipilih karena useful dan reusable;
- phrase models dapat diadaptasi anak;
- error direspons dengan natural recast/model.

### Evidence of participation
Anak menggunakan kata/frasa untuk mencapai tujuan nyata dalam permainan atau cerita.

---

## Phase 5 — INTERACT

**Child question:** “How do we communicate together?”

### Developmental focus
- turn-taking;
- merespons kontribusi orang lain;
- mengajukan/menjawab pertanyaan sederhana;
- menggunakan social language;
- mempertahankan exchange beberapa giliran;
- menggunakan bahasa dalam cooperative play.

### Rere experience standard
- percakapan bukan quiz;
- Rere harus benar-benar menanggapi kontribusi anak;
- ada ruang untuk initiation dari anak;
- language model berkembang melalui interaction.

### Evidence of participation
Anak bukan hanya menjawab prompt, tetapi mulai memulai, melanjutkan, atau mengubah arah interaksi.

---

## Phase 6 — COMMUNICATE

**Child question:** “How can we achieve something together?”

### Developmental focus
- menyampaikan ide;
- menjelaskan kebutuhan;
- mempertahankan interaksi lebih lama;
- menceritakan urutan kejadian sederhana;
- menjelaskan alasan sederhana;
- memecahkan masalah bersama;
- menegosiasikan pilihan sederhana;
- menyesuaikan komunikasi dengan konteks secara bertahap.

### Rere experience standard
- open-ended communication;
- lebih sedikit scripted response;
- lebih banyak choice, negotiation, storytelling, dan collaborative problem solving;
- familiar language digunakan dalam konteks baru.

### Evidence of participation
Anak menggunakan komunikasi untuk membangun makna bersama, bukan sekadar menghasilkan jawaban yang benar.

# 3. Three-Year Curriculum Arc

## YEAR 1 — Build the Communication Foundation

**Primary developmental arc:** NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND

Tujuan Year 1 bukan membuat anak “bisa bahasa Inggris”, tetapi membangun hubungan positif antara **bahasa, makna, tindakan, dan interaksi**.

### Priority
- listening comprehension;
- familiar vocabulary;
- simple functional language;
- response through action/choice/gesture/word;
- predictable routines;
- songs, stories, and repetition;
- confidence to participate;
- Indonesian as a secure communication anchor;
- initial English exposure.

### Typical experience design
**See/hear → understand → choose/act → hear again → optionally imitate/use.**

### English direction
English paling efektif ditempatkan pada language yang predictable dan highly contextual:
- greetings;
- actions;
- familiar objects;
- colors/size;
- simple routines;
- repeated story phrases;
- songs/chants.

---

## YEAR 2 — Use Language with Purpose

**Primary developmental arc:** RESPOND → EXPRESS → INTERACT

Fokus bergeser dari memahami dan merespons menuju **menggunakan komunikasi untuk mencapai tujuan**.

### Priority
- functional requests and refusals;
- describing;
- preferences;
- asking for help;
- simple questions and answers;
- short conversations;
- sequencing and retelling;
- cooperative play language;
- richer vocabulary networks;
- broader English phrase use.

### Typical experience design
**Situation → communication need → child response → Rere responds → child adapts/extends → repeat in new context.**

### English direction
English semakin sering digunakan sebagai functional language, tetapi home language tetap valid. Anak dapat berpindah bahasa sesuai kebutuhan dan konteks.

---

## YEAR 3 — Communicate, Narrate & Collaborate

**Primary developmental arc:** INTERACT → COMMUNICATE

Fokus bergeser menuju komunikasi yang lebih flexible, sustained, dan collaborative.

### Priority
- longer conversational exchanges;
- narrative sequence and retelling;
- sharing ideas and experiences;
- explaining simple reasons;
- collaborative problem solving;
- negotiation and choice;
- adapting language to situation;
- transferring familiar language to unfamiliar contexts;
- increasingly independent English use where meaningful.

### Typical experience design
**Shared situation → child idea → interaction → problem/choice → communication → shared outcome.**

### English direction
English dapat menjadi medium yang lebih substantial dalam episode tertentu ketika context, visuals, repetition, and predictability cukup kuat. Full-English tetap format pilihan, bukan requirement.

---

# 4. Strand Progression Across Three Years

| Learning Strand | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listening & Comprehension | familiar words/instructions | descriptions, questions, story events | multi-step/simple implied meaning |
| Vocabulary | recognize and name useful words | combine and reuse words | select/adapt vocabulary by context |
| Functional Communication | request/choose/respond | describe, ask, refuse, help | explain, negotiate, collaborate |
| Conversation | take a turn | maintain short exchange | sustain and shape interaction |
| Narrative | identify events | sequence/retell | retell with detail and reshape/share |
| Sound & Phonological Awareness | notice/imitate sounds | distinguish/play with rhyme and rhythm | manipulate/play with more complex sound patterns |
| Multilingual/English | contextual exposure | functional use alongside home language | flexible use and transfer |

---

# 5. English Developmental Arc

SPEAK uses a separate English lens inside the broader communication progression:

**HEAR → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → USE → GENERALIZE**

The three-year relationship is:

### Year 1 — Exposure & Comprehension
**Hear → Understand**

English is attached to concrete objects, actions, routines, stories, songs, and predictable interaction.

### Year 2 — Functional Use
**Respond → Use**

Children encounter English as a tool for requests, descriptions, choices, play, and familiar social interaction.

### Year 3 — Transfer & Flexibility
**Use → Generalize**

Familiar English is reused in new contexts, with increasing independence when appropriate.

> Imitation remains an important learning technique across all three years, but it is never treated as the final developmental goal.

---

# 6. Complexity Model

SPEAK should vary complexity through four dimensions rather than simply adding more vocabulary.

### A. Context complexity
**Concrete/familiar → familiar variation → less predictable → new context**

### B. Response complexity
**Action/gesture → choice → word → phrase → connected response**

### C. Interaction complexity
**Single turn → turn-taking → short exchange → sustained exchange → collaborative interaction**

### D. Language flexibility
**Model → imitate → adapt → initiate → transfer**

This prevents curriculum growth from becoming “more words, longer scripts” only.

---

# 7. Developmental Spiral

Important communication functions should recur throughout all three years.

Example: **asking for help**

- Year 1: recognize “help” and respond to “Can you help?”
- Year 2: use “Help me, please.” / “Can you help me?”
- Year 3: explain what help is needed and negotiate how another person can help.

Example: **describing**

- Year 1: “red”, “big”;
- Year 2: “It is a big red ball.”;
- Year 3: compare, explain, and use description to solve a shared task.

Example: **storytelling**

- Year 1: identify character/action;
- Year 2: sequence and retell;
- Year 3: add details, explain what happened, or reshape the story.

The same language function should therefore **return with greater agency, complexity, and transfer**.

---

# 8. What Progress Should NOT Mean

Rere should not define progress as:

- memorizing more vocabulary;
- speaking only English;
- producing longer sentences at all times;
- perfect pronunciation;
- perfect grammar;
- answering every question correctly;
- reducing use of Bahasa Indonesia;
- performing on command for adults.

A better definition is:

> **Progress = the child increasingly understands and uses communication to participate, express meaning, interact, and accomplish things with others.**

---

# 9. Curriculum Design Implications

When creating a SPEAK episode, curriculum planners should ask in order:

1. **What communication intention are we developing?**
2. **What does the child need to understand?**
3. **What meaningful reason does the child have to respond?**
4. **What response forms are acceptable at this developmental level?**
5. **How will Rere respond to the child's contribution?**
6. **Where will the language repeat with variation?**
7. **Can the child encounter the same function outside this episode?**
8. **Is English serving communication, rather than becoming the objective by itself?**

---

# 10. Readiness Standard for Future Curriculum

A curriculum sequence is developmentally coherent when:

- Year 1 builds comprehension and participation before demanding production;
- Year 2 turns language into a practical communication tool;
- Year 3 expands interaction, narrative, collaboration, and transfer;
- important functions spiral instead of appearing once;
- English exposure grows through meaningful use rather than forced English-only progression;
- home language remains respected throughout;
- increasing complexity comes from context, response, interaction, and flexibility—not vocabulary volume alone;
- age remains a reference rather than a gate.

This document is a **roadmap**, not an episode inventory. Specific themes and episodes should be derived later through the Rere curriculum development method.
