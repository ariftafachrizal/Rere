**Target audience:** 2–4 years

> This slate is a production candidate, not a claim that all six episodes are already script-locked. Episode 001 is the current development baseline; Episodes 002–006 enter development only after the batch learning progression is approved.

## 1. Batch Strategy

The first batch should establish Rere's educational grammar through familiar, visually strong concepts.

Recommended progression:

**Recognize → Match → Compare → Act → Apply → Create**

The batch should feel like one coherent learning world while allowing each episode to stand alone.

## 4. EP-002 — Rere Mencari Bentuk

### Learning promise
Anak mengenali bentuk sederhana melalui benda familiar.

### Candidate vocabulary
- lingkaran
- persegi
- segitiga

### Candidate story
Rere ingin membuat gambar dari bentuk-bentuk sederhana. Ia mencari benda di playroom yang memiliki bentuk berbeda.

### Participation
- “Mana yang bentuk lingkaran?”
- “Bisa tunjuk yang segitiga?”
- matching shape to object

### Candidate objects
- ball / circular object
- square block
- triangular toy/sign
- shape cards

### Progression
Recognize → Match.

### Important design rule
Do not teach too many shapes in the first episode. Three shapes are the preferred starting scope.

## 5. EP-003 — Rere Belajar Angka 1–5

### Learning promise
Anak mengenali dan menghitung jumlah 1–5 dengan benda konkret.

### Candidate vocabulary
satu, dua, tiga, empat, lima.

### Candidate story
Rere ingin menyiapkan lima mainan untuk bermain dan menghitungnya satu per satu.

### Participation
- count together
- point while counting
- identify quantity

### Candidate objects
- blocks
- balls
- crayons
- toy fruits

### Progression
Recognize → Count → Act.

### Safety / pedagogy
Use one-to-one correspondence visually. Avoid presenting too many objects at once.

## 6. EP-004 — Rere Memilih dan Mencocokkan

### Learning promise
Anak mencocokkan benda yang sama atau memiliki atribut yang sama.

### Candidate story
Rere menemukan beberapa benda yang tercampur dan ingin memasangkan benda yang cocok.

### Candidate activities
- same color matching
- same shape matching
- object-to-object matching

### Participation
“Mana yang sama?”

### Progression
Recognize → Match → simple Compare.

### Important design rule
The task must remain visually simple; use a small number of choices per round.

## 7. EP-005 — Rere Belajar Bagian Tubuh

### Learning promise
Anak mengenali dan menyebutkan bagian tubuh dasar.

### Candidate vocabulary
mata, hidung, mulut, tangan, kaki.

### Candidate story
Rere bermain gerak tubuh sederhana sambil mengenali bagian tubuh.

### Participation
- point to body part
- imitate movement
- name body part

### Example interaction
“Mana mata Rere?”

[PAUSE]

“Ini mata!”

### Progression
Recognize → Name → Imitate.

### Safety
Keep language factual, simple, and body-positive. Avoid unnecessary anatomical detail.

## 8. EP-006 — Rere Rapikan Mainan

### Learning promise
Anak mengenal kebiasaan sederhana merapikan dan mengelompokkan mainan.

### Candidate vocabulary
rapikan, simpan, mainan, kotak, selesai.

### Candidate story
Setelah bermain, Rere melihat mainannya masih berserakan. Rere mengajak teman-teman menyimpan mainan bersama.

### Participation
- pick up
- sort
- place
- repeat routine

### Progression
Recognize → Act → Apply.

### Primary value
Kemandirian dan tanggung jawab sederhana.

## 12. Batch-Wide Creative Rules

- Same Rere identity in every episode.
- Same playroom geography when playroom recurs.
- Variation comes from activity, object, camera, story beat, and learning goal—not from redesigning Rere.
- Use consistent opening and closing.
- Use the same Rere voice identity.
- Keep CTA treatment consistent.
- Avoid making every episode visually identical; vary composition and action while preserving the visual language.
- No artificial danger or fear.
- No excessive stimulation.
