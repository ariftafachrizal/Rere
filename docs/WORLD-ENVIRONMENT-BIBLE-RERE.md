# Rere — World & Environment Bible

**Status:** Working Production Standard — v1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Scope:** visual/video, storyboard, background, animation, thumbnails, title cards, and scene production.

## 1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mengunci dunia tempat Rere hidup, bermain, belajar, dan bercerita. Tujuannya bukan membuat semua background identik, tetapi memastikan seluruh lokasi mempunyai **visual DNA yang sama**.

> **Character consistency menjawab “Apakah ini tetap Rere?” World consistency menjawab “Apakah ini tetap dunia Rere?”**

## 2. Core World Principle

> **The world changes for the story; the visual language stays Rere.**

Lokasi boleh berpindah dari rumah, taman, sekolah, kebun, dapur, market, komunitas, alam, hingga dunia imajinasi. Visual language harus tetap terasa sebagai universe Rere.

## 3. World Hierarchy

Ketika terdapat konflik:

1. Rere Character Identity
2. Learning Intention
3. Story Function
4. Environment Identity
5. Props & Set Dressing
6. Camera & Lighting
7. Decorative Details

Background tidak boleh lebih dominan daripada Rere atau tujuan belajar.

## 4. Rere World DNA

Default environment harus terasa:

- warm
- cheerful
- soft
- clean
- playful
- child-safe
- rounded
- imaginative
- inviting
- preschool-friendly
- educational without looking institutional

Dunia harus terasa seperti **dunia anak**, bukan ruang dewasa yang diperkecil.

## 5. Shape, Material & Texture Language

### Shape

Gunakan rounded corners, soft curves, simple geometry, friendly silhouettes, dan low visual aggression.

Hindari dominasi sharp industrial geometry, gothic shapes, intimidating structures, dan harsh architecture.

### Materials

Default:

- light wood
- matte painted surfaces
- soft fabric
- paper
- rounded plastic toys
- natural soft materials

Material realistis boleh digunakan jika diperlukan, tetapi tidak boleh mengubah mood menjadi cold/adult.

### Texture

Texture memberi depth secukupnya.

**Readable → Warm → Detailed**

bukan **Detailed → Busy → Distracting**.

## 6. Color System

Pink adalah signature color Rere, tetapi **seluruh dunia tidak boleh menjadi pink**.

Pink digunakan sebagai accent pada:

- furniture detail
- textiles
- signage
- flowers
- toys
- small decorative elements

Supporting palette:

- cream
- warm white
- light wood
- pastel blue
- pastel green
- pastel yellow
- soft lavender
- gentle neutrals

> **Pink identifies Rere; supporting colors make the world livable.**

## 7. Lighting DNA

Default:

- soft
- warm
- diffused
- bright enough for preschool readability
- gentle highlights
- soft shadows
- natural-looking

Indoor default: soft morning/daylight.

Bedtime: warm, cozy, calm, low-intensity but still readable.

Hindari harsh contrast, horror lighting, extreme neon, gloomy darkness, dan overexposure.

## 8. Scale & Child Perspective

Environment harus dirancang dari perspektif anak:

- meja dan kursi child-size
- rak buku mudah dijangkau
- toys berada di level anak
- step stool/wastafel masuk akal
- playground sesuai preschool

Jangan membuat rumah dewasa lalu hanya mengecilkan furniture.

# 9. Canonical Locations

## 9.1 Playroom — World Anchor

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`

Home base utama untuk opening, closing, general learning, toys, storytelling, art, puzzle, dan basic concepts.

**Anchor wajib:**

- soft pastel wall
- light wood furniture
- play rug
- low bookshelf
- small learning table
- toy storage
- selected toys
- small plant
- window dengan soft daylight
- subtle pink accents
- heart/flower details
- optional framed Rere artwork

Major layout harus konsisten ketika ruangan muncul kembali.

## 9.2 Bedroom

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-BEDROOM-01`

Untuk bedtime, morning routine, self-care, emotional stories, dan quiet storytelling.

DNA: cozy, cream, light wood, simple bed, small bookshelf, plush bunny, soft rug, gentle lamp, soft pink accents.

Tidak boleh terasa seperti kamar remaja atau luxury bedroom.

## 9.3 Kitchen

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-KITCHEN-01`

Untuk cooking, food vocabulary, healthy habits, counting ingredients, dan life skills.

DNA: rounded cabinetry, light wood, cream surfaces, pastel accents, child-safe learning counter.

Dangerous appliances harus berada dalam konteks aman/adult supervision bila diperlukan.

## 9.4 Garden

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-GARDEN-01`

Untuk plants, nature, colors, science, responsibility, dan outdoor exploration.

DNA: green plants, flowers, soft sunlight, rounded garden elements, small watering can, child-sized tools, selective pink accents.

## 9.5 Classroom

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-CLASSROOM-01`

Untuk school stories, group learning, social skills, literacy, dan numeracy.

DNA: bright, welcoming, child-scale furniture, books, art display, learning wall, simple educational props.

Tetap play-based, bukan classroom formal yang intimidating.

## 9.6 Playground / Park

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-OUTDOOR-PLAY-01`

Untuk gross motor, social play, friendship, nature, dan outdoor discovery.

DNA: trees, grass, flowers, rounded playground equipment, safe paths, open sky, soft daylight.

## 9.7 Street / Community

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-COMMUNITY-01`

Untuk community helpers, safety, manners, transportation, dan social awareness.

Harus bersih, aman, child-friendly. Traffic/safety tidak dibuat fear-based.

## 9.8 Shop / Market

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-MARKET-01`

Untuk counting, colors, food, social interaction, dan simple money concepts.

Signage dan item harus mudah dibaca anak; jangan terlalu padat.

## 9.9 Beach / Nature

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-NATURE-01`

Untuk nature, weather, animals, sensory exploration, dan environmental awareness.

Warna dapat mengikuti alam, tetapi visual DNA Rere tetap dipertahankan.

## 9.10 Imagination World

**ID:** `RERE-WORLD-IMAGINATION-01`

Untuk CREATE, pretend play, fantasy, storytelling, dan problem solving.

Boleh lebih magical/colorful, tetapi face, proportions, wardrobe identity, dan acting language Rere tetap canonical.

# 10. Environment Continuity

Jika lokasi berulang, pertahankan:

- architecture
- major furniture
- window/door placement
- primary color relationships
- major set pieces
- recurring anchor props
- scale
- lighting direction bila waktunya sama

AI tidak boleh secara random mengubah playroom menjadi ruangan lain.

## 11. Prop Hierarchy

### Anchor Props

Membantu mengenali lokasi: bookshelf, play rug, learning table, toy storage.

### Story Props

Diperlukan episode: crayons, apple, ball, watering can, magnifying glass.

### Decorative Props

Memperkaya visual tanpa mengambil fokus.

Prioritas:

**Story/Anchor > Decoration**.

## 12. Prop Density

> **Few meaningful objects > many random objects**

Untuk audience 2–4 tahun, setiap scene idealnya mempunyai:

- 1 primary focal object
- beberapa supporting objects
- limited decoration

## 13. Camera & Environment

**Close-up:** background sederhana.  
**Medium:** cukup environment untuk konteks.  
**Full body:** lantai dan ruang gerak terlihat.  
**Wide:** establishing/location shot bila membantu cerita.

Gunakan tiga depth layers:

1. Foreground — optional
2. Midground — Rere + primary action
3. Background — environment anchors

## 14. Time of Day

- Morning — energetic discovery
- Daytime — primary learning
- Afternoon — play/outdoor
- Sunset — transition/emotional
- Night — bedtime/calm

Continuity harus mengalahkan aesthetic randomness.

## 15. Weather

Allowed default states:

- sunny
- cloudy
- light rain
- after rain
- gentle wind

Extreme weather hanya bila mempunyai learning/story purpose yang jelas.

## 16. Safety

Hindari random:

- exposed dangerous electrical outlets
- unsafe sharp furniture
- accessible chemicals
- hazardous machinery
- unsafe traffic
- frightening environmental elements

Jika bahaya menjadi topik pembelajaran, gunakan **learning opportunity + safe behavior**, bukan fear spectacle.

## 17. Cultural & Muslimah Compatibility

Regular dan Muslimah Rere berada dalam **universe yang sama**.

Muslimah-compatible settings dapat mencakup:

- modest home setting
- family setting
- Ramadan/Eid decoration
- mosque/community setting bila relevan
- halal food context

Jangan membuat Muslimah world menjadi franchise visual yang berbeda.

## 18. Storybook / Educational Visualization

Rere dapat masuk ke:

- storybook world
- imagination world
- simplified educational visualization
- magical learning space

Transition harus membantu anak memahami bahwa ini adalah imajinasi/visualisasi konsep.

## 19. AI Generation Anchor

```text
Set the scene inside the established Rere universe.
Preserve Rere's warm, cheerful, soft, playful, preschool-friendly visual language.
Use rounded child-scale forms, soft pastel supporting colors,
light wood, warm cream neutrals, gentle daylight, clean composition,
and selective pink accents as Rere's signature color.
The environment should feel like a coherent recurring world,
not a generic children's background.
Maintain continuity of architecture, major furniture, scale,
lighting direction, and anchor props when continuing the same location.
Keep Rere as the visual focal point and do not let background detail compete with her.
```

## 20. Negative Prompt — World Drift

```text
Do not create a generic random children's background.
Do not change the established Rere visual language.
Do not make the environment overly realistic, dark, gloomy, or intimidating.
Do not make the environment look like an adult space scaled down.
Do not overcrowd the scene with random toys or decorations.
Do not use excessive neon colors.
Do not make pink dominate every surface.
Do not change recurring room architecture between shots.
Do not randomly move major furniture.
Do not introduce unsafe child environment elements without narrative purpose.
Do not let background detail overpower Rere.
Do not create visually inconsistent worlds between adjacent shots.
```

## 21. Environment ID & Versioning

Format:

`RERE-WORLD-[LOCATION]-[VERSION]`

Examples:

- `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`
- `RERE-WORLD-BEDROOM-01`
- `RERE-WORLD-KITCHEN-01`
- `RERE-WORLD-GARDEN-01`
- `RERE-WORLD-CLASSROOM-01`

Jika canonical layout berubah secara intentional, naikkan version dan catat decision.

## 23. Environment QA Checklist

### World Identity

- [ ] Terasa sebagai dunia Rere.
- [ ] Visual DNA konsisten.
- [ ] Pink menjadi accent, bukan seluruh background.
- [ ] Rounded dan child-friendly.

### Continuity

- [ ] Architecture konsisten.
- [ ] Furniture utama konsisten.
- [ ] Scale konsisten.
- [ ] Major props konsisten.
- [ ] Time/lighting masuk akal.

### Story

- [ ] Environment mendukung learning intention.
- [ ] Primary object jelas.
- [ ] Background tidak mengganggu.
- [ ] Props memiliki fungsi/alasan.

### Safety

- [ ] Child-safe.
- [ ] Tidak ada bahaya random.
- [ ] Safety scenes tidak fear-based.

### Character

- [ ] Rere tetap focal point.
- [ ] Environment tidak mengubah identity.
- [ ] Regular dan Muslimah berada dalam universe yang sama.
