# EP006 — RERE BELAJAR MERAPIKAN MAINAN

**Storyboard V01 — Production Lock Draft**  
**Target:** ages 2–4, 3–4 minutes  
**Pillar:** LIVE  
**Learning promise:** setelah bermain, mainan diambil dan dikembalikan ke tempatnya.

## Canonical Sources
Canonical Rere character/face/outfit; `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`; canonical recurring props when used; stable balls and blocks; two fixed storage destinations.

## Shot Plan

### S01 — Opening | 0:00–0:12
Rere greets in playroom. FS, child-height. Exact canonical opening.

### S02 — Messy-but-safe Playroom | 0:12–0:30
A manageable amount of toys remains after play; no dangerous clutter. Rere: “Wah, kita sudah selesai bermain. Lihat... mainannya ada di mana-mana! Yuk, kita rapikan bersama!”

### S03 — Pick One | 0:30–0:52
Rere picks up exactly one toy and holds it clearly. “Ambil satu mainan.” PAUSE 3 sec. “Bagus! Sekarang kita simpan.”

### S04 — Correct Places | 0:52–1:18
Rere puts ball in fixed basket, block in fixed box. Show each mapping separately. No simultaneous confusing action.

### S05 — Repeat Routine | 1:18–1:45
Rere repeats take→carry→put away with one item. “Simpan di tempatnya.” Camera stays stable.

### S06 — Child Participation | 1:45–2:30
Rere: “Sekarang, lihat mainanmu. Ada yang bisa kamu rapikan?” PAUSE 4–5 sec. “Yuk, ambil... lalu simpan.”

### S07 — Review | 2:30–3:00
Before/after view. Rere: “Selesai bermain, kita rapikan. Ambil, simpan, selesai!”

### S08 — Achievement | 3:00–3:18
Rere admires organized playroom. “Playroom Rere sudah rapi. Hebat sekali!” Gentle reward sound.

### S09 — Closing | 3:18–3:55
Centered Rere, canonical closing/end card.

## Gemini Constraints
Storage destinations must remain fixed and physically accessible. Toy movement must be visible and believable; no magical disappearance. Reject unsafe clutter, inaccessible storage, moving furniture, prop redesign, character drift, random props, text artifacts and overstimulation.
