# D-026 — SPEAK Three-Year Curriculum Roadmap

**Status:** Approved Working Roadmap — 2026-09-12

## Decision

Rere adopts a three-year SPEAK curriculum roadmap built from the approved developmental progression:

> **NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → EXPRESS → INTERACT → COMMUNICATE**

The roadmap is a developmental map rather than a rigid syllabus, assessment framework, or age gate.

## Three-Year Arc

- **Year 1 — Build Communication Foundation:** NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND
- **Year 2 — Use Language with Purpose:** RESPOND → EXPRESS → INTERACT
- **Year 3 — Communicate, Narrate & Collaborate:** INTERACT → COMMUNICATE

## Supporting Decisions

1. English follows the parallel pathway **HEAR → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → USE → GENERALIZE**.
2. Communication remains the primary goal; English is an important language pathway.
3. Home-language, mixed-language, nonverbal, and English responses may all represent meaningful participation.
4. Progress is measured through meaningful communication, not vocabulary count or English-only performance.
5. Complexity grows through context, response, interaction, and language flexibility.
6. Important communication functions spiral across the three years.
7. Episode creation starts from Primary Learning Intention, not from a topic or activity.
8. The roadmap remains flexible and can change based on development, evidence, production learning, safety, and audience learning.

## Validation

The roadmap was stress-tested against 40 realistic communication, multilingual, English, story, sound/rhythm, social interaction, safety, emotion, play, and transfer cases.

**Result: PASS. No structural change required.**
