# Rere — CREATE Three-Year Curriculum Roadmap

**Status:** Approved Working Curriculum — 2026-09-12

## Purpose

Roadmap ini menerjemahkan CREATE developmental progression menjadi arah pengalaman belajar tiga tahun. Ini adalah peta, bukan daftar episode yang dikunci.

Structure:

**3 Years = MAP → 1 Year = JOURNEY → Quarter = CHAPTER → Episode = EXPERIENCE**

CREATE tetap mengikuti prinsip **Stable architecture, flexible curriculum**.

---

# Year 1 — Explore & Express

**Dominant progression:** EXPLORE → IMITATE → VARY

### Chapter 1 — My Marks, Sounds & Moves

Fokus:
- coregulated exploration of marks;
- scribble and simple drawing;
- making sounds with voice/body/safe objects;
- simple movement and freeze games;
- copying a short rhythm or movement.

Early literacy/numeracy may appear naturally through marks, symbols, counting beats, shapes, and sequences, but CREATE remains primary when expression/creation is the intention.

### Chapter 2 — Pretend Worlds

Fokus:
- symbolic substitution;
- pretend cooking/shop/doctor/home;
- caring for dolls/toys;
- animal role play;
- using ordinary objects in imaginative ways.

### Chapter 3 — Little Stories

Fokus:
- choosing a character;
- choosing what happens next;
- completing a simple unfinished story;
- making a character sound or movement;
- acting out a familiar routine with a playful change.

### Chapter 4 — Color, Shape, Rhythm & Choice

Fokus:
- choose colors/materials;
- vary a repeated mark or movement;
- simple sound patterns;
- simple building and rebuilding;
- combine two familiar creative actions.

**Year 1 destination:** the child experiences creative activity as a safe place to explore and make choices.

---

# Year 2 — Choose & Create

**Dominant progression:** VARY → CREATE

### Chapter 1 — Mix & Make

Fokus:
- combine colors/shapes/materials;
- create simple collages or constructions;
- make something from a small set of open materials;
- change a first idea and try again.

### Chapter 2 — Pretend Problems

Fokus:
- pretend shop with a problem;
- pretend doctor/caregiver scenarios;
- travel/adventure play;
- choose roles and events;
- invent simple solutions inside pretend worlds.

### Chapter 3 — Build a Story

Fokus:
- beginning-middle-end;
- create a character;
- create a simple problem;
- choose or invent a solution;
- make an alternative ending;
- turn a picture/object set into a story.

### Chapter 4 — Make a Pattern, Make a Move

Fokus:
- create simple rhythm patterns;
- combine clapping/stomping/movement;
- create short dances;
- change one part of a song/game;
- lead a simple repeated pattern.

**Year 2 destination:** the child increasingly creates something that reflects their own choices and ideas.

---

# Year 3 — Create, Share & Extend

**Dominant progression:** CREATE → SHARE & EXTEND

### Chapter 1 — Ideas That Grow

Fokus:
- develop one idea through several steps;
- revise a creation;
- add, remove, transform, rebuild;
- explain or demonstrate how an idea changed.

### Chapter 2 — Stories We Can Change

Fokus:
- reshape plots;
- add characters;
- create problems and multiple solutions;
- change endings;
- combine two story ideas;
- co-create with caregiver/peer.

### Chapter 3 — Music We Make Together

Fokus:
- create and repeat patterns;
- combine rhythms;
- invent movement sequences;
- take turns leading;
- respond to another person's musical/movement idea;
- change tempo/sequence in playful ways.

### Chapter 4 — Build, Pretend & Collaborate

Fokus:
- shared construction;
- pretend environments;
- collaborative role play;
- simple constraints that invite adaptation;
- share, respond, extend, and negotiate creative ideas.

**Year 3 destination:** the child can increasingly sustain, share, transform, and co-develop creative ideas.

---

# Cross-Year Spiral

CREATE should repeatedly revisit the same experiences with increasing agency and complexity:

| Experience | Early | Developing | Extending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drawing | Explore marks | Combine and vary | Build scenes/ideas and transform them |
| Pretend play | Symbolic substitution | Short scenario | Sustained collaborative world |
| Story | Choose an event | Build sequence | Reshape and extend narrative |
| Rhythm | Copy | Vary | Create/combine/lead |
| Movement | Imitate | Change | Create and transform sequence |
| Making | Build/rebuild | Make with intention | Develop/adapt/extend |

# Six-Month Initial Content Direction

The first six months should not attempt to cover the whole CREATE curriculum. The priority is to establish Rere's **creative participation language and recurring formats**.

Suggested format families:

1. **Rere Makes...** — open-ended drawing/making
2. **What Happens Next?** — story creation
3. **Pretend With Rere** — imaginative role play
4. **Make a Move** — movement creation
5. **Copy, Change, Create** — rhythm/sound/movement variation
6. **Build It Your Way** — open-ended construction
7. **Finish the Story** — narrative completion/variation
8. **What Should We Add?** — extend a creation

These are format families, not fixed episode requirements.

# Curriculum Design Rules

Every CREATE episode should specify:

- Primary Pillar = CREATE;
- one Primary Learning Intention;
- Core Area;
- expected child agency/choice;
- child response/creation opportunity;
- simplest entry point;
- optional extension;
- supporting pillars, if any;
- safety/material considerations;
- whether the experience is physical, imaginative, narrative, musical, visual, or mixed.

### Agency test

Ask:

> **"Apa yang benar-benar dipilih atau diciptakan anak?"**

If the answer is "nothing—the child only watches Rere make something," the episode is not sufficiently participatory for a CREATE-first experience.

### Product test

Ask:

> **"Apakah hasil yang berbeda tetap dianggap berhasil?"**

If no, the activity is probably too product-directed.

### Integration test

Ask:

> **"Apakah supporting pillar memperkuat kreativitas, atau justru mengambil alih tujuan?"**

If the latter, change the Primary Pillar.

# Safety & Accessibility

CREATE activities must support multiple ways of participating:

- voice;
- gesture;
- movement;
- drawing/mark-making;
- pretend play;
- choosing;
- pointing;
- building;
- storytelling;
- observation followed by later participation.

For craft/making:
- materials must be age-appropriate;
- avoid small parts where choking risk is relevant;
- avoid sharp/hot/toxic materials for independent child use;
- caregiver supervision is required when an activity needs it;
- video instructions must never imply that supervision can be replaced by the screen.

---

# Success Signals

Rere should learn from audience behavior without turning creativity into a formal assessment.

Useful signals:
- child joins a repeated action;
- child makes a choice;
- child varies the model;
- child initiates an idea;
- child repeats the activity outside the episode;
- caregiver reports child-created extensions;
- child returns to a recurring format;
- child shares or co-creates with another person.

Avoid using:
- correct/incorrect creative answers;
- artwork quality scores;
- imitation accuracy as the main metric;
- forced verbal explanation as proof of creativity.

---

# Roadmap Governance

The roadmap may be revised when:
- developmental evidence changes;
- repeated audience observation shows a format is too passive or too difficult;
- safety review identifies a problem;
- a new recurring format proves more effective;
- Rere's audience expands to older developmental stages.

The architecture and quality principles should remain more stable than the episode inventory.
