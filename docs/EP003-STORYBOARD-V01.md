# EP003 — RERE BELAJAR ANGKA 1–5

**Storyboard V01 — Production Lock Draft**  
**Target:** ages 2–4, 3–4 minutes  
**Pillar:** DISCOVER  
**Learning promise:** menghitung benda konkret 1–5 dengan one-to-one correspondence.

## Canonical Sources
Character/face/outfit canonical references; `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`; canonical Numeracy Learning Sheet; one stable family of five countable toys; optional number cards 1–5.

## Shot Plan

### S01 — Opening | 0:00–0:12
Rere greets in canonical playroom. FS, child-height. Exact canonical opening.

### S02 — Count Mission | 0:12–0:28
Five identical toys are visible. Rere says: “Rere menemukan mainan! Yuk kita hitung bersama!” Camera WS→MS. Ensure all five are countable and unobstructed.

### S03 — One and Two | 0:28–0:50
Rere places toy #1 then #2 in a clear row. “Satu... dua!” Each toy receives exactly one count word. Hands and objects remain visible.

### S04 — Three | 0:50–1:12
Rere adds toy #3. “Satu, dua, tiga!” Brief PAUSE before the child is prompted to answer. No hidden fourth/fifth toy in the active count area.

### S05 — Four | 1:12–1:34
Rere adds toy #4. “Satu, dua, tiga, empat!” PAUSE. Maintain exact object geometry and positions.

### S06 — Five | 1:34–1:58
Rere adds toy #5. “Satu, dua, tiga, empat, lima!” PAUSE. Camera widens enough to show all five.

### S07 — Child Participation | 1:58–2:40
Five toys visible. Rere: “Sekarang giliran kamu. Ada berapa mainan?” PAUSE 4–5 sec. Then: “Ayo hitung bersama: satu, dua, tiga, empat, lima!”

### S08 — Review | 2:40–3:10
Same five toys and optional 1–5 number support. Rere repeats sequence. No new quantity or counting trick.

### S09 — Closing | 3:10–3:45
Rere celebrates, heart gesture, canonical closing/end card.

## Gemini Constraints
The exact count is a production invariant. Every generation prompt must specify which objects are visible and their intended count. Reject duplicate/hidden objects, object-shape drift, floating objects, changing toy design, unreadable numbers, extra props, character drift, or visual clutter.
