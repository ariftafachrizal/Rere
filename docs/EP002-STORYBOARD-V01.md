### S02 — Shape Mission | 0:12–0:28
**Visual:** Rere discovers three shape pieces on activity table.  
**Dialogue:** “Rere mau membuat gambar! Tapi... Rere butuh beberapa bentuk. Yuk cari!”  
**Action:** Rere points to the shape set.

### S03 — Circle | 0:28–0:48
**Visual:** circle isolated and large enough to read immediately; Rere traces its curved edge.  
**Dialogue:** “Wah, lihat! Ini bentuk lingkaran. Seperti roda! Mana yang bentuknya lingkaran?”  
**PAUSE:** 4 sec.  
**Answer:** “Betul! Lingkaran.”  
**Camera:** MS→CU learning object.

### S04 — Square | 0:48–1:08
**Visual:** square isolated; Rere points around its four sides.  
**Dialogue:** “Ini bentuk persegi. Sisinya rapi. Mana yang bentuknya persegi?”  
**PAUSE:** 4 sec.  
**Answer:** “Betul! Persegi.”

### S05 — Triangle | 1:08–1:28
**Visual:** triangle isolated; three corners clearly visible.  
**Dialogue:** “Lihat! Ini bentuk segitiga. Ada tiga sisi. Mana yang bentuknya segitiga?”  
**PAUSE:** 4 sec.  
**Answer:** “Betul! Segitiga.”

### S06 — Match | 1:28–1:55
**Visual:** three shape pairs arranged left-to-right. Rere physically moves each shape beside its identical counterpart.  
**Dialogue:** “Sekarang kita cocokkan! Lingkaran cocok dengan lingkaran. Persegi cocok dengan persegi. Segitiga cocok dengan segitiga.”  
**Rule:** one-to-one matching; no ambiguous distractors.

### S07 — Create | 1:55–2:35
**Visual:** Rere makes a simple picture using circle, square and triangle.  
**Dialogue:** “Yuk bikin gambar dari bentuk! Lingkaran... persegi... segitiga!”  
**Motion:** calm, tactile, no rapid cuts.

### S08 — Review | 2:35–3:05
**Visual:** all three shapes displayed. Rere points one by one.  
**Dialogue:** “Hari ini kita belajar lingkaran, persegi, dan segitiga.”  
**Action:** child can name along.

### S09 — Closing | 3:05–3:40
**Visual:** centered Rere, heart gesture.  
**Dialogue:** exact canonical closing.  
**End card:** `BELAJAR • BERMAIN • BERBAGI / BERSAMA RERE!`
