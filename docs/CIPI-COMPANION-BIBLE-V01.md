# CIPI — COMPANION BIBLE V01

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED NAMING LAYER

## 1. Identity

**Name:** Cipi  
**Descriptor:** Kelinci Pintar  
**Role:** Rere's primary companion character  
**Visual identity:** the existing canonical Pink Bunny asset `RERE-PROP-PINK-BUNNY-01`

Cipi is the named character represented by the existing canonical pink bunny. Naming does not authorize redesign of the canonical bunny asset.

## 2. Brand Role

Cipi is part of the public duo brand:

> **Rere dan Cipi**

Rere is the main host/learning companion. Cipi is the curious companion who helps create interaction, reaction, humor, modeling and participation.

Cipi is **not** a second teacher and must not compete with the primary learning intention.

## 3. Personality

Cipi is:

- curious
- cheerful
- friendly
- playful
- brave to try
- sometimes unsure
- willing to learn
- expressive
- kind

Cipi may make simple, safe mistakes so children have opportunities to notice, think and respond. Mistakes must never create shame, fear or misleading learning.

## 4. Meaning of “Kelinci Pintar”

“Pintar” does not mean Cipi always knows the answer.

Cipi is “pintar” because Cipi:

- suka mencari tahu;
- berani mencoba;
- mau bertanya;
- mau memperbaiki kesalahan;
- belajar bersama Rere dan anak.

## 5. Canonical Visual Rule

The existing canonical Pink Bunny reference remains the source of truth for:

- face
- ears
- body proportions
- colors
- silhouette
- bow treatment
- eyes and expression language
- approved anatomy

Scene generation may change pose, expression, camera, action and environment, but must not silently redesign identity.

## 6. Interaction Pattern

Preferred interaction:

**Rere/Cipi → Question or discovery → PAUSE → Child responds → Rere/Cipi reacts → Reinforcement**

Cipi can be used for:

- asking a simple question
- making a guess
- noticing something
- demonstrating an action
- celebrating a correct response
- modeling retrying after a mistake

## 7. Dialogue Style

Cipi's dialogue should be:

- short
- natural
- child-friendly
- expressive
- easy to understand
- Indonesian-first

Avoid long explanations, adult vocabulary, sarcasm, humiliation and forced comedy.

## 8. Brand Usage

Public brand:

> **Rere dan Cipi**

Use “Cipi” as the normal character reference.

Use “Cipi — Kelinci Pintar” when introducing the character, describing the brand, or explaining the character role to parents.

“Pink bunny” / “kelinci pink” is a visual descriptor, not the character's public name.

## 9. QA

Reject or revise if:

- Cipi visually drifts from the canonical bunny.
- Cipi becomes the primary teacher when Rere should lead.
- Cipi repeatedly gives misleading answers.
- Cipi's mistakes shame or confuse children.
- Cipi's personality changes without a documented character decision.
- Cipi disappears from brand packaging where the duo is intended to be visible.
