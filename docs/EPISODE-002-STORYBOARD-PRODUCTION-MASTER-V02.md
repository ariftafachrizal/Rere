## 0. PURPOSE

Blueprint produksi visual untuk Gemini. Karakter, dunia, dan prop canonical yang sudah approved adalah **source of truth**; storyboard hanya menentukan pengalaman, action, timing, camera, dan continuity.

**Experience:** Rere bermain mencari dan mencocokkan bentuk bersama satu anak.

**Learning flow:** NOTICE → TOUCH/TRACE → NAME → QUESTION → PAUSE → REVEAL → MATCH → REPEAT.

**Learning target:** lingkaran, persegi, segitiga. Anak tidak perlu mempelajari istilah geometri formal; cukup mengenali bentuk dan pasangan yang sama.

---

# 1. CANONICAL LOCKS

- Character: canonical Rere character/face/anatomy/outfit.
- World: `RERE-WORLD-PLAYROOM-01`.
- Props: canonical Learning Book, Crayon Kit and approved shape-learning assets where used.
- Shape assets: approved circle, square, triangle; geometry must remain identical in all shots.
- Camera: child-height, gentle, readable.
- Lighting: canonical warm daytime playroom lighting.
- No redesign of any canonical asset at shot level.

---

# 2. GLOBAL VISUAL RULES

- One primary learning shape at a time during teaching shots.
- Shape must be large enough for a 2–4-year-old to recognize immediately.
- Use simple solid colors or restrained pastel contrast; color must never be the matching criterion when shape is being taught.
- Background must not contain other objects with the same silhouette that could create ambiguity.
- Rere's hands and the complete shape must remain visible during interaction.
- No floating pieces, morphing geometry, impossible stacking, or magically appearing objects.
- Motion level 1–2 except the simple creation sequence.
- Question/pause must have a stable visual frame.

---

# 3. CONTINUITY MAP

The entire episode occurs in the same playroom, same daytime period. Rere begins near the familiar playroom entry/central area and moves toward the learning table. Keep table, rug, bookshelf, storage and window geography consistent. Shapes should be stored/placed on the same learning surface once introduced.

| Shape | Canonical learning object | Teaching cue | Match cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Circle | circle piece/card | round edge | same round silhouette |
| Square | square piece/card | four straight sides | same square silhouette |
| Triangle | triangle piece/card | three corners | same triangular silhouette |

# 4. SHOT-BY-SHOT MASTER

## S01 — SIGNATURE OPENING

**Audio:** canonical opening sonic identity; light playful bed.

## S02 — SHAPE MISSION HOOK
**Time:** 0:12–0:28  
**Camera:** WS → MS, gentle follow

**Purpose:** Establish the mission: Rere needs shapes for a simple picture.

**Action chain:** Rere walks to learning table → sees Learning Book → opens it → notices three shape pieces/cards → looks curious.

**Composition:** three shapes visible as a group but not yet taught simultaneously; Rere remains dominant.

**Gemini instruction:** Canonical playroom continuity. Show Rere naturally discovering the approved three shape assets beside the canonical Learning Book. Keep all three shapes stable, simple and clearly separated. Do not introduce additional shapes or random craft materials.

**Reject if:** shape count changes, geometry drifts, clutter, oversized props, impossible hand interaction.

## S03 — CIRCLE
**Time:** 0:28–0:48  
**Camera:** MS → CU learning area  
**Motion:** 1

**Learning objective:** recognize and say “lingkaran”.

**Action chain:** Rere selects circle → traces edge once with finger → holds it flat toward camera → looks at child/camera → asks → holds still during pause → reveals.

**Dialogue:** “Ini bentuknya bulat. Namanya apa, ya?”

**PAUSE:** 4 sec.

> “Lingkaran!”

> “Ling-kar-an. Ayo bilang bersama Rere.”

**Critical visual:** during pause the circle remains fully visible; Rere must not cover its silhouette.

**Audio:** music ducks for question/pause; small reward chime after answer.

**Gemini instruction:** Use approved circle reference. Rere gently traces the circular edge, then presents the exact same shape clearly at child height. Hold the composition stable during the question and waiting moment. No other circular learning object in focus.

**Reject if:** circle becomes oval, polygonal, partially hidden, duplicated, or visually confused with another object.

## S04 — SQUARE
**Time:** 0:48–1:08  
**Camera:** same learning preset; slight lateral continuity move  
**Motion:** 1

**Learning objective:** recognize and say “persegi”.

**Action chain:** Rere places circle in its stable area → reaches for square → traces one side and corner → presents square → question → 4 sec pause → reveal.

**Dialogue:** “Kalau yang ini, bentuknya apa?”

**PAUSE:** 4 sec.

> “Persegi!”

> “Per-se-gi. Pintar!”

**Composition:** square face-on to camera; all four sides visible.

**Gemini instruction:** Continue exact playroom and Rere continuity. Use approved square reference without changing corner radius/geometry. Present the square front-facing and unobstructed. Avoid other square objects in the learning plane.

**Reject if:** rectangle-like proportions, rounded transformation, competing square shapes, object clipping.

## S05 — TRIANGLE
**Time:** 1:08–1:28  
**Camera:** MS → CU  
**Motion:** 1

**Learning objective:** recognize and say “segitiga”.

**Action chain:** Rere moves square aside → picks up triangle → gently traces two sides and points to three corners → holds it clearly → question → pause → reveal.

**Dialogue:** “Yang ini punya tiga sudut. Namanya apa?”

**PAUSE:** 4 sec.

> “Segitiga!”

**Critical visual:** exactly three visible corners; no extra triangular motifs near the object.

**Gemini instruction:** Use the approved triangle reference exactly. Rere gently indicates the three corners without excessive pointing. Keep the triangle fully visible during pause. Maintain simple preschool-friendly geometry.

**Reject if:** four or more corners, distorted triangle, multiple triangles, answer visually leaked by background.

## S06 — MATCH THE SHAPES
**Time:** 1:28–1:55  
**Camera:** WS → slightly top-down learning CU  
**Motion:** 1–2

**Learning objective:** match identical shapes one-to-one.

**Action chain:** Rere places three source shapes on left → three identical targets on right → matches circle → square → triangle one at a time.

**Dialogue:** “Sekarang kita cari pasangan yang sama. Lingkaran dengan lingkaran... persegi dengan persegi... segitiga dengan segitiga. Cocok!”

**Composition:** six pieces in a clean 2×3 or two-column arrangement. Each target has only one plausible partner.

**Audio:** three soft placement sounds, one per correct match.

**Gemini instruction:** Generate a clean matching layout using only the approved circle, square and triangle assets. Six pieces total: exactly three pairs. Keep identical partners visually identical in geometry and scale. Rere moves one piece at a time and places it beside its identical partner. No ambiguous distractors.

**Reject if:** two possible matches, shape changes after placement, pieces overlap, extra pieces appear.

## S07 — CREATE A SIMPLE PICTURE
**Time:** 1:55–2:35  
**Camera:** MS with slight elevated learning angle  
**Motion:** 2

**Purpose:** transfer recognition into playful creation.

**Action chain:** Rere uses circle + square + triangle to build a very simple picture (e.g. house/character-like object) on the Learning Book → points to each shape as she uses it → admires result.

**Dialogue:** “Kita bisa membuat gambar dari bentuk-bentuk ini. Ini lingkaran... ini persegi... ini segitiga. Wah, jadi gambar!”

**Learning rule:** creation is reinforcement, not a new art lesson.

**Audio:** calm creation music, soft placement SFX.

**Gemini instruction:** Use only the three canonical shape geometries. Rere physically places them on the canonical Learning Book in a simple recognizable preschool composition. Shapes do not morph or become new forms. Keep hands physically coherent and actions sequential.

**Reject if:** shape morphing, overly complex artwork, impossible stacking, new shape types.

# 5. EDITING / TIMELINE RULES

- Keep cuts clean and easy to follow.
- Learning-object shots may use one motivated push-in; never rapid zoom.
- Hold 3–4 seconds for child response windows.
- Duck music during every question and pause.
- Use SFX only to clarify touch/place/match.
- Never cut away during the core question/pause.
- Match hand/object screen geography between consecutive learning shots.

# 6. GEMINI GENERATION PACKAGE

For every shot attach only the minimum required references in this order:
1. canonical Rere character;
2. face/expression if needed;
3. canonical outfit;
4. canonical playroom;
5. relevant canonical recurring prop;
6. approved shape asset;
7. this shot specification.

**Universal negative:** no character redesign, face drift, hairstyle/outfit drift, adult proportions, extra fingers/limbs/eyes, malformed hands, uncanny face, photorealism, scary mood, random props, world drift, prop geometry inconsistency, text artifacts, watermark/logo, clutter, excessive bloom, rapid camera movement, overstimulation.

# 7. QA GATE

P0/P1 issues are automatic reject. Specifically verify:
- exact canonical Rere identity;
- exact circle/square/triangle geometry;
- only intended shape visible during each learning pause;
- matching has one obvious answer;
- no shape morphing;
- child can see and understand the action;
- timing preserves every PAUSE;
- playroom continuity is stable.

**Golden rule:** If the child is confused, simplify. If Rere is inconsistent, regenerate. If shape geometry drifts, reject.
