# LIVE — Three-Year Curriculum Roadmap

**Status:** Approved Working Curriculum Map — 2026-09-12

## Year 1 — Participate in Everyday Life

**Progression:** NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE

Priorities:
- familiar hygiene and self-care routines;
- putting away belongings and participating in cleanup;
- simple eating/dressing routines;
- asking for help;
- basic boundaries and respectful interaction;
- predictable safety signals and trusted-adult support;
- simple reasons: clean, safe, helpful, caring.

Experience families:
- routine play;
- songs with actions;
- pretend home/shop/clinic play;
- simple safety stories;
- “help Rere” tasks;
- one-step or short-sequence participation.

## Year 2 — Contribute with Purpose

**Progression:** PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE

Priorities:
- longer routine sequences;
- shared-space responsibility;
- turn-taking and cooperation;
- basic safety decisions in familiar contexts;
- simple food/self-care participation with appropriate adult supervision;
- helping others;
- beginning to explain why a behavior matters;
- adapting when small routine changes occur.

Experience families:
- cooperative cleanup;
- pretend cooking/shop;
- routine problem stories;
- safe-choice games;
- helping characters;
- role-play of waiting, asking, and contributing.

## Year 3 — Apply & Take Increasing Responsibility

**Progression:** PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY

Priorities:
- multi-step familiar routines;
- simple planning and preparation;
- recognizing risks across varied contexts;
- adapting routines when circumstances change;
- contributing to group goals;
- making safe choices within firm boundaries;
- explaining simple reasons and consequences;
- transferring skills to new situations.

Experience families:
- multi-step mission/play scenarios;
- shared project routines;
- changing-context safety stories;
- pretend community roles;
- plan-do-check activities;
- collaborative responsibility challenges.

## Curriculum Strands Across Three Years

| Strand | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Values & respect | notice/model | practice | apply/adapt |
| Life skills | participate | sequence/contribute | increasingly independent |
| Safety | recognize | choose safely in familiar context | recognize/adapt across contexts |
| Responsibility | small contribution | shared responsibility | simple planning |
| Agency | safe choice | choose method/order | plan/adapt within boundaries |
| Healthy habits | participate | connect action to purpose | transfer across contexts |

## Spiral Themes

Revisit:

**cleaning → hygiene → dressing → eating routines → helping → caring for shared spaces → safety → responsibility → contribution**

Complexity increases through:

**sequence + context + independence + reasoning + social responsibility**

## Episode Mapping

Each LIVE episode defines:

1. Primary Learning Intention;
2. primary LIVE Core Area;
3. developmental reference;
4. real-life/play context;
5. action the child can perform;
6. reason/meaning;
7. safety boundaries if relevant;
8. support/scaffolding;
9. optional supporting pillars;
10. repetition/transfer opportunity.

## Initial Production Guidance

For the first 6 months, prioritize high-frequency routines and safety/value situations children encounter repeatedly. Avoid creating episodes merely to enumerate rules. A strong episode gives the child a reason to act, a chance to participate, and a path to reuse the skill.

## Success Definition

A strong LIVE curriculum should help children increasingly:

> **care for themselves, respect and contribute to life with others, recognize important safety boundaries, and use practical skills with growing understanding and independence.**
