# Rere — CREATE Three-Year Developmental Progression

**Status:** Approved Working Framework — 2026-09-12

## Identity

**CREATE — Imagine, Express & Make**

CREATE membantu anak membayangkan, mengekspresikan, mencoba, dan menciptakan sesuatu dengan caranya sendiri.

Progression utama:

**EXPLORE → IMITATE → VARY → CREATE → SHARE & EXTEND**

Progression ini adalah arah perkembangan desain pengalaman belajar, bukan assessment formal dan bukan cutoff usia.

---

## 1. Three-Year Arc

### Year 1 — Explore & Express

Dominant movement:

**EXPLORE → IMITATE → VARY**

Fokus:
- mencoba media, suara, gerakan, benda, dan pretend play sederhana;
- meniru contoh pendek tanpa tuntutan hasil yang sama;
- memilih dari pilihan sederhana;
- mulai mengubah warna, suara, gerakan, benda, atau bagian cerita;
- menikmati pengalaman kreatif sebagai permainan.

Outcome arah perkembangan:

> Anak mulai memahami bahwa ia dapat melakukan sesuatu dengan caranya sendiri.

### Year 2 — Choose & Create

Dominant movement:

**VARY → CREATE**

Fokus:
- membuat pilihan kreatif lebih mandiri;
- menggabungkan beberapa ide/material/gerakan;
- membuat pretend play dengan alur sederhana;
- menciptakan karakter, kejadian, pola ritme, gerakan, gambar, atau bentuk sendiri;
- mulai mempertahankan sebuah ide melalui beberapa langkah;
- mulai berbagi hasil dan menjelaskan secukupnya dengan dukungan.

Outcome arah perkembangan:

> Anak mulai menggunakan kreativitas untuk menghasilkan ide dan ekspresi yang berasal dari dirinya sendiri.

### Year 3 — Create, Share & Extend

Dominant movement:

**CREATE → SHARE & EXTEND**

Fokus:
- mengembangkan ide menjadi karya/aksi/cerita yang lebih utuh;
- mengubah atau menggabungkan ide secara fleksibel;
- membuat dan memodifikasi cerita;
- menciptakan pola musik/gerak dan memvariasikannya;
- menggunakan pretend play untuk kolaborasi;
- berbagi ide dan menerima kontribusi orang lain;
- memperpanjang permainan atau karya melalui pertanyaan dan kemungkinan baru;
- memindahkan strategi kreatif ke konteks baru.

Outcome arah perkembangan:

> Anak semakin mampu menghasilkan, membagikan, mengembangkan, dan menyesuaikan ide kreatif bersama orang lain.

## 2. Progression by Core Area

| Core Area | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative Expression | Explore media, imitate, make simple choices | Combine, vary, create own expression | Develop, transform, share and extend expression |
| Pretend Play & Imagination | Symbolic substitutions and simple role play | Build short scenarios and choose roles/events | Sustain, combine and negotiate imaginative play |
| Storytelling & Narrative Creation | Choose/complete simple event or character | Create sequence, character, problem or ending | Build, reshape and extend narrative with others |
| Music, Rhythm & Movement | Imitate sounds/rhythms/movements | Vary and create simple patterns | Create, combine, lead and transform patterns |
| Open-Ended Making | Explore/build/rebuild | Plan a simple idea and modify it | Develop, adapt and extend constructions/creations |

## 3. Complexity Dimensions

CREATE progression should not be measured only by how complicated the final product looks. Increase complexity across several dimensions:

### A. Choice complexity
**One choice → several choices → open choice → connected choices**

### B. Idea complexity
**Single action → combination → sequence → connected idea → evolving idea**

### C. Transformation
**Copy → small variation → multiple variations → transform → invent**

### D. Persistence
**Brief exploration → repeat → continue an idea → revise → sustain/extend**

### E. Social complexity
**Create beside others → share → create together → respond to another's idea → collaborate/shape together**

### F. Representation
**Gesture/sound → symbolic object → drawing/building → story/role-play → multi-modal expression**

### G. Flexibility
**Accept one way → choose another → change when needed → combine alternatives → adapt creatively to new context**

These dimensions can progress independently. A child may show advanced imagination while still needing simple materials or adult support.

---

## 4. Engagement Ladder

Rere uses:

**WATCH → COPY → CHOOSE → CREATE → SHARE**

The ladder describes participation, not child quality.

- **WATCH:** child attends to the invitation.
- **COPY:** child joins by imitating.
- **CHOOSE:** child makes a meaningful choice.
- **CREATE:** child produces or transforms an idea.
- **SHARE:** child shows, tells, performs, or continues the idea with another person.

Not every episode must reach SHARE. Rere should progressively create more opportunities for children to move toward agency and creation.

## 5. Examples of Developmental Spirals

### Drawing
- Year 1: scribble, explore marks, imitate a simple mark, choose a color.
- Year 2: combine shapes/marks, add intentional elements, decide what the picture represents.
- Year 3: create a scene, add characters/events, explain or extend the picture into a story.

### Pretend shop
- Year 1: imitate giving/taking and symbolic use of objects.
- Year 2: choose what the shop sells and act out a short scenario.
- Year 3: invent roles, problems, solutions, rules and interactions with another player.

### Story
- Year 1: choose a character or what happens next.
- Year 2: create beginning-middle-end with support.
- Year 3: reshape the plot, add a problem/solution, change the ending, or combine another person's idea.

### Music and movement
- Year 1: copy clap, stomp, freeze, or animal movement.
- Year 2: change one element and create a simple pattern.
- Year 3: create, combine, lead, repeat, and transform a rhythm or movement sequence.

### Building
- Year 1: stack, connect, knock down, rebuild.
- Year 2: make a simple intended object or place and modify it.
- Year 3: develop a structure for a purpose, encounter a constraint, adapt, and extend the design.

## 6. What Progress Does NOT Mean

CREATE progress is **not**:

- more beautiful artwork;
- more realistic drawing;
- more expensive materials;
- copying Rere more accurately;
- longer crafts;
- perfect rhythm or dance technique;
- producing a predefined final product;
- speaking more during every creative activity;
- always working independently;
- following every instruction correctly.

Better definition:

> **Progress = the child increasingly makes choices, transforms ideas, expresses meaning, sustains exploration, and creates with growing agency.**

---

## 7. Caregiver Scaffolding Progression

Adult support should gradually shift from demonstration toward collaboration and release of control:

**SHOW → INVITE → OFFER OPTIONS → ASK OPENLY → FOLLOW CHILD'S IDEA → CO-CREATE → STEP BACK**

The appropriate level depends on age, familiarity, material, safety, and the individual child.

Caregiver support should never turn an open-ended activity into an adult-controlled craft assignment.

---

## 8. Developmental Governance

Age is a reference point, not a gate. Rere should:

- allow multiple entry points into the same episode;
- provide simpler and more challenging variations;
- avoid labeling children as behind because their creative expression differs;
- treat repetition as valuable;
- preserve child choice where safety allows;
- avoid turning the progression into a score or checklist.

The three-year progression provides the **direction**. Actual episode design should remain flexible and responsive to observed audience needs.
