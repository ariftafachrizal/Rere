## 0. PURPOSE

Episode mengenalkan lima kosakata tubuh yang sangat dasar: **mata, hidung, mulut, tangan, kaki**. Anak diajak menunjuk/meniru gerakan sederhana, bukan mempelajari anatomi atau fungsi medis.

**Learning flow:** LOOK → POINT → NAME → QUESTION → PAUSE → IMITATE → REPEAT.

**Safety principle:** seluruh visual tetap child-safe, non-clinical, non-medical, tidak menampilkan bagian tubuh secara terpisah atau dengan cara yang menakutkan.

---

# 1. CANONICAL LOCKS

- Canonical Rere character, face and anatomy.
- Regular canonical outfit.
- Preferred world: `RERE-WORLD-BEDROOM-01` with approved child-scale mirror; playroom may be used if the mirror reference is canonical there.
- Mirror geometry and reflection must remain spatially coherent.
- No new anatomy design at shot level.

---

# 2. GLOBAL VISUAL RULES

- Rere remains fully clothed in canonical preschool outfit.
- Body-part cues are gentle: point to eyes, nose, mouth; wave hands; move feet.
- No close-ups that feel clinical.
- Face remains friendly and expressive.
- Keep both hands/feet physically correct.
- During pauses, the target body part must be visible and clearly indicated without obstructing it.
- Use mirror as an optional reinforcement, never as a source of distorted identity.

---

# 3. CONTINUITY MAP

Rere starts beside the canonical child-scale mirror in the same bedroom. She remains in the same outfit and lighting. The mirror stays in one fixed position. For eyes/nose/mouth, use MCU/CU that still shows enough of Rere's face to feel natural. For hands/feet, widen to keep the relevant limb visible.

**Time:** 0:12–0:28  
**Camera:** WS → MS  
**Motion:** 1–2

## S03 — EYES / MATA
**Time:** 0:28–0:50  
**Camera:** MCU → gentle CU on face  
**Motion:** 0–1

**Learning objective:** recognize and say “mata”.

**Action:** Rere looks at camera → gently points with one finger near her eyes without covering them → asks → PAUSE → touches/indicates both eyes → smiles.

**Dialogue:** “Mana mata Rere?”

**PAUSE:** 4 sec.

> “Ini mata!”

> “Ma-ta. Yuk tunjuk mata.”

**Critical visual:** both eyes remain clearly visible. Finger does not block the eyes.

**Gemini:** canonical face reference is mandatory. Preserve exact eyes, eyelashes, nose and lips. Rere makes a gentle near-eye pointing gesture. Mirror reflection, if visible, must match exactly.

**Reject:** changed eyes, extra eyes, finger covering entire face, clinical close-up, distorted reflection.

## S04 — NOSE / HIDUNG
**Time:** 0:50–1:12  
**Camera:** MCU → CU

## S05 — MOUTH / MULUT
**Time:** 1:12–1:34  
**Camera:** MCU → CU  
**Motion:** 0–1

> “Mu-lut. Senyum!”

**Action after answer:** Rere gives a small smile, not an exaggerated mouth movement.

**Gemini:** preserve canonical small smiling lip shape. Keep mouth child-friendly and natural. No teeth emphasis or uncanny lip motion.

**Reject:** lip shape drift, exaggerated mouth, talking-mouth artifacts, extra teeth, clinical framing.

## S06 — HANDS / TANGAN
**Time:** 1:34–1:58  
**Camera:** MS, both upper limbs visible  
**Motion:** 2

**Reject:** extra fingers/arms, merged fingers, hands cropped, impossible wrist rotation, excessive motion.

## S07 — FEET / KAKI
**Time:** 1:58–2:22  
**Camera:** MLS/full-body with slight lower emphasis

**Safety:** no jumping from furniture, no running, no unstable pose.

**Reject:** extra legs/feet, foot clipping, dangerous movement, camera too low, body distortion.

# 5. EDITING / AUDIO RULES

- No medical imagery, anatomy diagrams, X-rays or isolated body parts.
- Keep voice warm and slow enough for imitation.
- Duck music during every question and PAUSE.
- Use tiny gesture SFX only; no cartoon impact sounds.
- Avoid rapid face close-up cuts.
- Mirror shots should cut on natural movement and preserve spatial direction.

# 6. GEMINI PACKAGE

Attach: canonical Rere → canonical face/expression → regular outfit → canonical bedroom/mirror → body-learning reference → shot specification.

**Universal negative:** no character redesign, face drift, hairstyle/outfit drift, adult proportions, extra fingers/limbs/eyes, malformed hands/feet, medical imagery, isolated body parts, photorealism, scary mood, uncanny mirror, reflection mismatch, random props, text artifacts, watermark/logo, clutter, excessive camera motion.

# 7. QA GATE

- [ ] Five target words are correct and consistent.
- [ ] Each body part is clearly indicated.
- [ ] Rere remains fully clothed and child-safe.
- [ ] Mirror reflection is exact.
- [ ] No anatomy errors.
- [ ] PAUSE windows are preserved.
- [ ] Child can imitate every requested action safely.

**Golden rule:** teach the body through friendly play, never through clinical imagery.
