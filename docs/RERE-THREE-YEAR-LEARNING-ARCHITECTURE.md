# Rere — Three-Year Learning Architecture

**Status:** Approved Working Architecture — 2026-09-12

## 1. Purpose

Dokumen ini menyatukan lima Core Pillars Rere menjadi satu sistem pembelajaran yang koheren untuk anak usia dini. Arsitektur ini menjadi jembatan antara philosophy, developmental progression, curriculum, episode design, dan production.

Rere tidak dibangun sebagai lima mata pelajaran. Lima pillar adalah **learning lenses** yang dapat digunakan dalam satu pengalaman bermain tanpa kehilangan primary intention.

## 2. Learning Philosophy

Rere adalah teman belajar yang mengubah screen time menjadi pengalaman bermain yang bermakna.

Core learning loop:

**PLAY → EXPERIENCE → IMITATE → LEARN**

Integrated child experience:

**NOTICE → PARTICIPATE → EXPLORE → EXPRESS → CONNECT → CREATE → APPLY**

Tidak semua episode harus mengikuti seluruh urutan. Urutan ini menggambarkan perjalanan belajar yang ingin semakin sering dialami anak.

## 3. Five Core Pillars

| Pillar | Identity | Core Question |
|---|---|---|
| **DISCOVER** | Understand the World | Apa yang terjadi? Bagaimana hubungan/kerjanya? |
| **SPEAK** | Communicate with the World | Bagaimana aku memahami dan menyampaikan makna? |
| **FEEL** | Understand Myself & Others | Apa yang aku rasakan dan bagaimana kita saling memahami? |
| **LIVE** | Care for Myself, Others & My World | Bagaimana aku hidup, menjaga, dan berkontribusi? |
| **CREATE** | Imagine, Express & Make | Apa yang bisa kubayangkan, ubah, dan ciptakan? |

### DISCOVER
**NOTICE → CONNECT → WONDER → INVESTIGATE → REASON → APPLY**

Primary territory: understanding the world, recognition/classification, patterns/relationships, early mathematical thinking, early literacy foundations, cause-effect, problem solving, natural/physical exploration, attention/memory/curiosity.

### SPEAK
**NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → EXPRESS → INTERACT → COMMUNICATE**

Primary territory: listening/comprehension, functional vocabulary, functional phrases, conversation/social communication, story/narrative language, sound/rhythm awareness, multilingual and English communication.

English pathway:
**HEAR → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → USE → GENERALIZE**

### FEEL
**NOTICE → NAME → REGULATE WITH SUPPORT → INTERACT → CARE → BEGIN TO CHOOSE**

Primary territory: emotional recognition/naming, co-regulation and coping, social interaction, empathy and early perspective-taking, self-awareness, flexibility, frustration tolerance, repair.

### LIVE
**NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → PARTICIPATE → INDEPENDENTLY APPLY**

Primary territory: values/respect, daily life skills/independence, safety, responsibility/contribution, healthy habits, agency and decision making.

### CREATE
**EXPLORE → IMITATE → VARY → CREATE → SHARE & EXTEND**

Primary territory: creative expression, pretend play/imagination, storytelling/narrative creation, music/rhythm/movement, open-ended making.

## 4. The Primary Learning Intention Rule

> **Activity does not determine the pillar. Learning intention determines the pillar.**

Every episode must declare:

1. **Primary Pillar** — exactly one.
2. **Primary Learning Intention** — one observable learning purpose expressed in child-development terms.
3. **Supporting Pillars** — zero or more, only when naturally useful.
4. **Cross-Domain Capabilities** — capabilities genuinely activated by the experience.

Supporting pillars must never compete with the primary intention.

### Example: Blocks

- Recognize shapes → DISCOVER
- Count blocks meaningfully → DISCOVER
- Find why a tower falls → DISCOVER
- Build a tower in a chosen way → CREATE
- Play/coordinate with a friend → FEEL
- Clean up together → LIVE
- Use "big block", "my turn" → SPEAK

The same activity can legitimately move between pillars.

## 5. Cross-Domain Capability Layer

These are not a sixth pillar.

### Thinking
Curiosity, attention, memory, problem solving, exploration.

### Doing
Fine motor, gross motor, coordination, participation.

### Self
Agency/choice, persistence, self-regulation.

### Social
Communication, cooperation, social interaction, relationship building.

### Creative
Imagination, expression, experimentation.

**PLAY** is the mechanism that connects the capability layer to the five pillars.

## 6. Literacy & Numeracy

Early literacy and early numeracy are **cross-domain foundations**, not standalone pillars.

They may be primary when the learning intention fits a pillar, but they should not be forced into every episode.

Examples:

- DISCOVER: quantity, patterns, classification, symbols, sounds, books.
- SPEAK: vocabulary, narrative, number words, sequencing language.
- FEEL: language for experience, turn-taking, sequencing social interaction.
- LIVE: signs, routine sequences, counting in real activities.
- CREATE: stories, symbols, patterns, shapes, construction.

## 7. Developmental Architecture

Rere uses age as a **reference, not a gate**.

The three-year roadmap describes increasing complexity rather than rigid age requirements.

### Year 1 — Participate & Build Foundations

Dominant experience: familiar context, repetition, modeling, concrete exploration, supported participation.

Typical progression across pillars:
- DISCOVER: notice/connect
- SPEAK: notice/understand/respond
- FEEL: notice/name/regulate with support
- LIVE: notice/understand/practice
- CREATE: explore/imitate

### Year 2 — Explore, Express & Contribute

Dominant experience: choice, variation, functional communication, social practice, simple reasoning, contribution.

Typical progression:
- DISCOVER: connect/wonder/investigate
- SPEAK: respond/express/interact
- FEEL: regulate with support/interact/care
- LIVE: practice/participate
- CREATE: imitate/vary/create

### Year 3 — Communicate, Apply & Create with Increasing Agency

Dominant experience: transfer, collaboration, flexible problem solving, narrative, adaptation, increasingly independent choices.

Typical progression:
- DISCOVER: investigate/reason/apply
- SPEAK: interact/communicate
- FEEL: interact/care/begin to choose
- LIVE: participate/independently apply
- CREATE: create/share/extend

These are developmental directions, not performance requirements.

## 8. Spiral Curriculum

Rere should revisit important concepts through different contexts rather than teach them once.

A concept can increase in complexity through:

1. **Context** — familiar → varied → new.
2. **Response** — action/gesture → choice → word → phrase → connected response.
3. **Interaction** — single turn → turn-taking → short exchange → sustained collaboration.
4. **Agency** — follow model → choose → vary → initiate → adapt.
5. **Reasoning** — notice → connect → predict → explain/use.
6. **Independence** — observe → participate → practice → contribute → apply.

## 9. Integrated Episode Architecture

Every episode starts from a learning intention, not from an activity title.

### Episode Brief Structure

**A. Learning Identity**
- Primary Pillar
- Primary Learning Intention
- Developmental Reference / Complexity Level

**B. Experience Design**
- Theme/context
- Play mechanism
- Child action
- Rere role
- Caregiver role, if relevant

**C. Learning Integration**
- Supporting Pillars
- Cross-domain capabilities
- Literacy/numeracy opportunities, if natural
- English exposure/function, if relevant

**D. Interaction Design**
- Invitation
- Child response opportunity
- Processing/pause time
- Feedback/acknowledgement
- Variation
- Re-entry/repetition opportunity

**E. Safety & Trust**
- age/context safety
- emotional safety
- material/environment safety
- body autonomy and boundaries where relevant
- caregiver supervision requirement where relevant

**F. Completion**
- celebration/closure
- optional extension
- transfer to real life/play

## 10. Integrated Experience Patterns

The five pillars do not need one universal episode script. Each primary pillar has its own natural experience pattern.

- DISCOVER: **HOOK → NOTICE → INVITE → TRY → OBSERVE → VARY → DISCOVER → CELEBRATE**
- SPEAK: **MODEL → INVITE → PAUSE → RECEIVE → RESPOND → EXTEND → REPEAT WITH VARIATION**
- FEEL: **SITUATION → EMOTION → PAUSE → NAME → SUPPORT/MODEL → CHOICE → PRACTICE → CONNECT/CLOSE**
- LIVE: **SITUATION → NOTICE → WHY IT MATTERS → MODEL → INVITE → PRACTICE → CHOOSE/CONTRIBUTE → CLOSE**
- CREATE: **INVITATION → DEMONSTRATE → OPEN CHOICE → CREATE → SHARE → EXTEND**

These are design patterns, not mandatory scene-by-scene formulas.

## 11. What Integration Means

Integration means the child experiences learning as connected, not that every episode explicitly teaches every pillar.

A strong episode may have:

> **1 Primary Pillar + 1–2 Supporting Pillars + several natural capabilities**

A strong episode may also have only:

> **1 Primary Pillar + no Supporting Pillar**

Both are valid.

### Integration test

Ask:

1. Does the supporting element naturally occur in the play?
2. Does it strengthen the primary intention?
3. Would removing it make the experience meaningfully worse?
4. Is the child still free to participate rather than perform multiple targets?

If not, remove the integration.

## 12. Child Agency Model

Rere should progressively move children from passive viewing toward participation and creation.

**WATCH → JOIN → CHOOSE → DO → EXPRESS → CREATE → SHARE → TRANSFER**

This is an engagement direction, not an assessment scale.

The child may participate through:
- speech
- home language
- English
- gesture
- movement
- pointing/choosing
- facial expression
- pretend play
- drawing/making
- silence followed by later participation

## 13. Parent/Caregiver Layer

Rere serves two audiences:

- Child = viewer/user.
- Parent/caregiver = trust and decision audience.

Caregiver messaging should communicate that Rere:
- supports play rather than replaces play;
- values participation over performance;
- respects home language and family context;
- does not require expensive materials;
- does not replace caregiver supervision for physical/material activities;
- does not diagnose or promise clinical outcomes.

## 14. Curriculum Governance

The architecture is stable; curriculum remains flexible.

**3 Years = MAP → 1 Year = JOURNEY → Quarter = CHAPTER → Episode = EXPERIENCE**

A roadmap may be revised when new evidence, child development, audience observation, quality review, or strategic learning justifies a change.

Do not optimize the curriculum for topic coverage alone. Optimize for meaningful developmental experiences.

## 15. Production Governance

The approved learning architecture is the source of truth.

Workflow:

**Rere Framework → Episode Brief → Gemini Production → Human/Quality Review → Final Asset → Publish → Learn → Curriculum Refinement**

Gemini may generate production material, but must not redefine learning intentions, pillar boundaries, safety principles, or developmental standards.

## 16. Architecture Quality Bar

Before an episode moves to production, it should pass the integrated Learning Standard in `docs/LEARNING-STANDARD.md`.

At minimum:

- one clear primary learning intention;
- developmentally appropriate complexity;
- meaningful child participation;
- play remains visible;
- integration is natural, not overloaded;
- language load is controlled;
- agency is protected;
- mistakes are safe;
- emotional and physical safety are respected;
- repetition includes variation;
- transfer or real-world connection is possible where appropriate.

## 17. Final Architecture Statement

Rere's five pillars form one connected learning system:

> **DISCOVER helps the child understand the world.**
>
> **SPEAK helps the child communicate with the world.**
>
> **FEEL helps the child understand self and others.**
>
> **LIVE helps the child care for self, others, and the world.**
>
> **CREATE helps the child imagine, express, and make.**

Together, they support a child who is not only able to **know**, but also to **communicate, feel, live, and create**.

The architecture is intentionally broad enough to grow with the child while remaining simple enough to guide individual episode decisions.
