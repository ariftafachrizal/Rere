## 1. Storyboard Objective

Storyboard ini menerjemahkan Script Lock V01 menjadi shot yang siap diproduksi. Setiap shot harus mengikuti canonical hierarchy:

**Identity → Face → Anatomy → Outfit → Gesture/Expression → World → Props → Camera → Lighting → Scene Details**

No scene generation may replace or redesign a canonical asset.

## 3. Global Camera Rules

- Child-perspective framing; avoid adult eye-level dominance.
- Default lens feel: natural, mild perspective; no extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Camera movement is gentle and motivated.
- One primary focal object per learning shot.
- Keep Rere's face readable when she speaks.
- During PAUSE, hold composition long enough for the child to inspect and respond.
- No rapid zooms, whip pans, shaking, or spectacle-driven movement.
- Preserve spatial direction and room continuity.

## 4. Global Lighting Rules

- Use canonical playroom lighting preset.
- Soft warm daylight / diffused key.
- Gentle fill and soft contact shadows.
- Preserve Rere skin tone and signature pink.
- Learning-object colors must remain accurate and clearly distinguishable.
- No dramatic contrast or flashing light.

## 7. Motion Intensity

Use the project motion scale:

- Opening: **1–2 / 4**
- Hook: **1–2 / 4**
- Learning discovery: **1–2 / 4**
- Ball action: **2 / 4**
- Pink heart: **1 / 4**
- Quiz: **0–1 / 4**
- Drawing: **1–2 / 4**
- Closing: **1 / 4**

No shot should rely on rapid motion to retain attention.

## 8. Audio / Edit Cues

- Lower music during every question and PAUSE.
- Never place a sound effect over the answer word.
- Correct-answer cue follows the child's response window.
- Use gentle transitions; hard cuts are acceptable when spatially clear.
- Preserve breathing/micro-pauses in Rere voice.
- Subtitles, if used, must prioritize the target color word and remain readable.
